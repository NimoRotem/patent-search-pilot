{# Tabbed document view, modelled on iptorch.com's per-result card: one tab strip, one pane, content fetched only when a tab is picked. Following iptorch, a tab whose data is known to be absent server-side is NOT rendered at all (rather than rendered and then apologising) — and for sections whose presence is only known after /api/ref returns, the pane itself degrades to a plain "not available" line. These are DISCLOSURE buttons, not a WAI-ARIA tablist: they toggle one shared pane, there is no roving tabindex and no aria-controls, so `role="tab"` would promise a keyboard contract that is not implemented. Plain buttons + aria-expanded is the honest mapping, and it is also ~30 bytes/button cheaper. Clicks are handled by ONE delegated listener (see app.js) rather than 8 inline onclick attributes per card — with ~25 cards that attribute alone was several KB of the page. WEIGHT: this strip is the only thing on the card that scales with the tab count, so it is kept to bare attributes. Everything it opens is fetched on demand. #}
