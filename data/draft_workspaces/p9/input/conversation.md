### USER

Here is my invention.

A reusable split pipe clamp has a first semicircular frame half and a second semicircular frame half joined by a hinge at one end and a releasable latch at the other end. Three radial jaw carriages are distributed around the closed frame. Each carriage slides in a radial guide and carries a pivoting concave jaw pad that bears on a pipe without requiring a tool-specific pipe diameter.

A segmented cam ring is retained in an annular guide of the frame. One cam-ring segment is carried by each frame half. When the halves close, complementary drive faces at the hinge and latch ends couple the segments so that they move as one ring. Each jaw carriage has a follower received in a respective oblique slot of the cam ring. Closing the latch drives a wedge against a drive face of the cam ring and rotates the coupled ring through a limited stroke. The oblique slots translate that rotation into synchronized inward travel of all three jaw carriages. A spring biases each carriage outward when the latch is released.

The latch has a first position that holds the frame halves around the pipe without clamping and a second over-center position that drives the cam ring and clamps the pipe. A force-limiting spring stack lies between the latch handle and the wedge, so additional handle travel after all jaw pads contact the pipe compresses the spring stack rather than forcing one jaw farther than the others. A visible indicator pin moves relative to a fixed edge of the latch housing when the spring stack enters a predetermined compression range. The indicator reports clamp loading mechanically and does not measure pipe diameter.

To install the clamp, an operator opens the latch, swings the frame halves apart, places them around a pipe, closes the frame, moves the latch to the holding position, and then moves the latch to the over-center position. The coupled cam ring advances all jaw carriages until the pivoting pads seat on the pipe. To remove the clamp, releasing the latch unloads the spring stack and the carriage springs retract the jaws before the frame halves are opened.

The frame may carry a mounting boss outside the annular guide for supporting a sensor, tool, or conduit. The mounting boss does not participate in jaw synchronization. The disclosed essential relationship is that closure couples the ring segments and latch travel drives the coupled ring, while the oblique slots synchronize the radial jaw carriages and the force-limiting spring stack accommodates the remaining latch travel.
