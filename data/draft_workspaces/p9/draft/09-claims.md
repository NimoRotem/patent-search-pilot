1. A split pipe clamp comprising:

a first frame half and a second frame half joined at a hinge end by a hinge and releasably joined at a latch end by a latch, the first frame half and the second frame half defining, in a closed condition, a frame having an annular guide and three radial guides;

three jaw carriages, each jaw carriage being slidable radially in a respective one of the three radial guides and carrying a jaw pad, each jaw pad being pivotally mounted on the jaw carriage and having a concave bearing face directed inwardly of the frame;

a segmented cam ring received in the annular guide, the segmented cam ring comprising a first cam ring segment carried by the first frame half and a second cam ring segment carried by the second frame half, the first cam ring segment and the second cam ring segment having complementary coupling faces at the hinge end and complementary coupling faces at the latch end, the complementary coupling faces at the hinge end and the complementary coupling faces at the latch end engaging one another as the first frame half and the second frame half are closed such that in the closed condition the first cam ring segment and the second cam ring segment rotate together in the annular guide as a coupled ring;

three oblique slots in the segmented cam ring and a follower on each jaw carriage received in a respective one of the three oblique slots, each oblique slot being inclined to a radial direction of the frame such that rotation of the coupled ring through a stroke advances the three jaw carriages inwardly in synchronism;

a wedge movable by the latch and positioned to bear on a ring drive face of the segmented cam ring, such that movement of the latch drives the wedge against the ring drive face and rotates the coupled ring through the stroke;

the latch comprising a latch handle and a force-limiting spring stack in a drive train between the latch handle and the wedge, the force-limiting spring stack being arranged such that travel of the latch handle after all three jaw pads have contacted a pipe received in the frame compresses the force-limiting spring stack rather than advancing one of the jaw carriages farther inwardly than the others; and

three carriage return springs, each biasing a respective one of the jaw carriages outwardly, such that release of the latch permits the carriage return springs to retract the jaw carriages.

3. The split pipe clamp of claim 2, wherein the indicator pin is displaced by compression of the force-limiting spring stack independently of a diameter of the pipe received in the frame.

13. A method of clamping a pipe, comprising:

opening a latch of a split pipe clamp and swinging a first frame half and a second frame half of the split pipe clamp apart about a hinge at a hinge end of the split pipe clamp, a first cam ring segment being carried by the first frame half and a second cam ring segment being carried by the second frame half;

placing the first frame half and the second frame half around the pipe;

closing the first frame half and the second frame half about the hinge so that complementary coupling faces at the hinge end and complementary coupling faces at a latch end engage one another and couple the first cam ring segment and the second cam ring segment into a coupled ring received in an annular guide of the split pipe clamp;

moving the latch to a first position in which the first frame half and the second frame half are held around the pipe without the pipe being clamped;

moving a latch handle of the latch toward a second, over-center position, thereby driving a wedge against a ring drive face of the coupled ring and rotating the coupled ring through a stroke;

advancing three jaw carriages of the split pipe clamp inwardly in synchronism by movement of a follower of each jaw carriage in a respective oblique slot of the coupled ring, until a jaw pad pivotally mounted on each jaw carriage seats on the pipe; and

continuing to move the latch handle into the second, over-center position after all three jaw pads have seated on the pipe, thereby compressing a force-limiting spring stack in a drive train between the latch handle and the wedge rather than advancing one of the jaw carriages farther inwardly than the others.

14. The method of claim 13, further comprising observing displacement of an indicator pin relative to a fixed edge of a latch housing of the latch, the displacement indicating that the force-limiting spring stack has entered a predetermined compression range.

15. The method of claim 13, further comprising releasing the latch to unload the force-limiting spring stack, retracting the three jaw carriages outwardly under carriage return springs so that the three jaw pads lift from the pipe, and thereafter swinging the first frame half and the second frame half apart about the hinge and removing the split pipe clamp from the pipe.
