# FIG. 1

Plan view of the split pipe clamp closed around a pipe, viewed along the pipe axis. Black line work on white, well inside the drawing area.

The pipe 90 is seen end on at the centre of the drawing area as a circle with its interior blank. An annular frame body surrounds it, bounded by an inner circle spaced outward from the pipe 90 and by an outer circle. The ring-shaped space between the pipe 90 and that inner circle is open, crossed only by the jaw pads 40 and the inner ends of the blocks that carry them.

Two radial joint lines, at the left and at the right, divide the annular frame body into two semicircular bodies: the upper is the first frame half 10, the lower the second frame half 12.

At the left joint is the hinge 14, shown schematically as a small circle on that joint line between the inner circle and the outer circle.

At the right joint is the latch 16, shown schematically as a rectangular block outside the frame body bridging both frame ends, with the latch handle 50 reaching radially outward from that block as a straight bar, the handle's attitude shown schematically, drawn inclined toward the first frame half 10 to represent the latch in the over-center position.

Three blocks are spaced around the annular frame body, at the top, the lower left and the lower right, one on the first frame half 10 and two on the second frame half 12, these three circumferential positions and their distribution between the two frame halves being shown schematically. At each position the inner circle is interrupted by a radial opening and the block is shown schematically as reaching radially inward through that opening, its outer portion within the body and its inner end inside the inner circle.

Each block carries at its inner end a separate member, the jaw pad 40, joined at a small circle, a pivot shown schematically; its inner face is a concave arc meeting the pipe 90.

A short broken cutting-plane line lies in the upper part of the view on the radial centre line of the topmost block, its outer end just outside the outer circle and its inner end at the pipe 90. A short arrow at each end points horizontally to the right, both on the same side of the line, giving the direction in which FIG. 3 is viewed. Each end carries the numeral 3 as the section designation, just outside that end beside its arrow, so the line reads as line 3-3; it is a section designation rather than a reference numeral, and no leader line runs to it.

## Numerals shown on this figure

- 100 split pipe clamp: target the outer circle of the frame body at the upper left, away from the pipe 90.
- 10 first frame half: target well inside the upper semicircular body.
- 12 second frame half: target well inside the lower semicircular body.
- 14 hinge: target the small circle at the left joint.
- 16 latch: target the rectangular block bridging the right joint.
- 40 jaw pad: target well inside the member at the inner end of the topmost block.
- 50 latch handle: target well inside the straight bar reaching radially outward from the latch block.
- 90 pipe: target the interior of the central circle, inside the inner circle.
