# FIG. 3

Enlarged fragmentary section through one jaw carriage, taken on line 3-3 of FIG. 1, the cutting plane containing that carriage's radial direction and the frame axis, the view's right being the axial side from which FIG. 1 is viewed. Radially outward is toward the top; the axis runs left to right. Cut solid material carries section hatching; the frame body, the segmented cam ring 20, the jaw carriage 30 with its follower 32, and the jaw pad 40 each at a slant different from that of every other cut element beside it; black line work on white, well inside the drawing area. Shown schematically: the carriage return spring 44 and the way the oblique slot 34 appears as an opening through the segmented cam ring 20.

A hatched body in section spans the upper part of the view, formed with a groove and with a channel.

The groove is bounded by the body on its radially outer side above and on its radially inner side below, its proportions being a drawing convention shown schematically. That groove is the annular guide 18.

A hatched block is received in the groove between its walls above and below, held against radial movement and free to move circumferentially, the fit shown schematically. That block is the segmented cam ring 20. One rectangular opening is formed through that block, sized to receive the follower 32. That opening is the oblique slot 34, its position in the block shown schematically.

The channel opens at the underside of the body, radially inward. That channel is the radial guide 38.

A hatched block lies in the channel and fills its width, its lower end projecting below the body. That block is the jaw carriage 30. A post extends from the jaw carriage 30 into that opening, the position on the carriage from which it projects being a drawing convention shown schematically, its sides facing the sides of the opening, so the only open space left in the opening is around the post. That post is the follower 32.

A spring, drawn as a zigzag spring symbol, acts between the jaw carriage 30 and the frame body, urging that carriage upward, radially outward. That spring is the carriage return spring 44.

Below the body, a further body hangs from the lower end of the jaw carriage 30 on a small circle, a pivot shown schematically. That body is the jaw pad 40, its lower face a concave arc.

## Numerals shown on this figure

- 18 annular guide: target the groove wall above the block in it.

- 30 jaw carriage: target the hatching of the block in the channel.
- 32 follower: target well inside the post.
- 34 oblique slot: target the open space of the opening beside the post.
- 38 radial guide: target a side wall of the channel holding the jaw carriage 30.
- 40 jaw pad: target well inside the body below the jaw carriage 30.
- 44 carriage return spring: target the zigzag spring symbol.
