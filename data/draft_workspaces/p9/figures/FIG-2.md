# FIG. 2

Plan view of the segmented cam ring removed from the frame, its two segments drawn in the relative positions they occupy when coupled in the closed condition, viewed along the ring axis from the same side as FIG. 1, so the hinge end is at the left and the latch end at the right. Black line work on white, well inside the drawing area. Shown schematically: the form of the meeting surfaces at the two joints, and the positions and tilt of the three slots.

A flat annulus at the centre of the drawing area is the segmented cam ring 20, bounded by one inner circular boundary and one outer circular boundary. The region within the inner boundary is open, and the three slots open between the two boundaries. Features of the ring other than the two joints and the three slots are not designated in this view.

Two joints, one at the left and one at the right, divide the annulus into two arcuate segments: the upper is the first cam ring segment 22 and the lower is the second cam ring segment 24.

At the left joint the ends of the two segments meet along complementary surfaces facing circumferentially, the complementary coupling faces at the hinge end 26. At the right joint the two segment ends meet in the same way, the complementary coupling faces at the latch end 28.

Three elongated openings are formed in the annulus, one near the top, one at the lower left, one at the lower right. Each is an oblique slot 34, a long narrow opening lying within the annulus width and inclined to the radius at its own position, so one end is nearer the inner boundary and the other nearer the outer. The three are alike and all tilt the same way, so that turning of the ring in one sense moves all three followers inwardly; the handedness of the tilt, and the corresponding sense of ring rotation, are not specified and are shown schematically.
