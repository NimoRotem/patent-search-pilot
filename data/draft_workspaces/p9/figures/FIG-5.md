# FIG. 5

Enlarged fragmentary view of the frame at the mounting boss, parts shown in section on a plane containing the frame axis and corresponding in orientation to the plane of FIG. 3. Radially outward is toward the top of the view, and the axis direction runs left to right, the view's right being the axial side from which FIG. 1 is viewed, as in FIG. 3. Cut solid material carries section hatching; the frame body with the mounting boss 70 that is joined to it and the segmented cam ring 20 each carry hatching at a slant different from that of every other cut element it touches or lies beside in the view; black line work on white, all of it well inside the drawing area.

A hatched body in section spans the lower part of the view. A groove is formed in it, its proportions shown schematically, bounded by the body on its radially outer side above and its radially inner side below. That groove is the annular guide 18.

A hatched block is received in the groove between its walls above and below, held against radial movement and free to move circumferentially, the fit shown schematically. That block is the segmented cam ring 20.

A further block projects from the outer surface of the body and is joined to the body along its base, standing outside the groove, with no part of it reaching into the groove. It is drawn schematically as a plain rectangular block in section, hatched like the body, and its placement relative to the groove is likewise shown schematically. That projecting block is the mounting boss 70.

An upper block rests on the upper end of the mounting boss 70 and is joined to it. It is drawn schematically as a plain rectangle in outline without hatching, and its name is a semantic identifier that does not appear as drawing text. That upper block is the equipment 80.

## Numerals shown on this figure

- 18 annular guide: target the wall of the groove above the block that lies in it.
- 20 segmented cam ring: target well inside the hatching of the block lying in the groove.
- 70 mounting boss: target well inside the hatching of the block projecting above the body.
- 80 equipment: target well inside the rectangle resting on the mounting boss 70.
