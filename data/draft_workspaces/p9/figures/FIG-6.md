# FIG. 6

Enlarged fragmentary detail of the latch-end joint of the clamp, viewed along the frame axis from the same side as FIG. 1, with the wall of the frame nearest the viewer broken away over the region shown, the frame and the two cam ring segments being shown in section at the break. Here the circumferential direction of the frame runs vertically and radially outward is toward the right. Cut material carries section hatching; the upper frame body, the lower frame body and each cam ring segment at a slant different from that of every other cut element it touches, so the parting line and the segment end contact stay distinct. Black line work on white, well inside the drawing area. Shown schematically: the extent of the region, the proportions of the bodies, the form of the meeting surfaces, and the extent of the break at each body's radially outer side.

Two hatched bodies stand one above the other, filling the upper and lower parts of the view, with a parting line between them running the full width of the view. The upper body is the latch end of the first frame half 10 and the lower body is the latch end of the second frame half 12. Each body is bounded on the left by an upright edge, on the right by an irregular break line, and toward the parting line by a straight horizontal end face.

The annular guide 18 of each frame half appears as a groove opening toward the parting line, one in the upper body opening downward and one in the lower body opening upward. Each groove is set back from the left edge of its body, so a band of hatched frame material stands between the groove and that edge, and the wall of the groove on that side, its radially inner side, faces the block lying in the groove. The irregular break line of each body runs along the radially outer boundary of its groove, so the view shows nothing of what lies radially outward of the two blocks at this joint.

A solid block lies in each groove and reaches along it to the end face of its body without passing beyond it. The block in the upper body is the first cam ring segment 22 and the block in the lower body is the second cam ring segment 24. The two blocks are of the same width and stand in line.

At the parting line the end faces of the first cam ring segment 22 and the second cam ring segment 24 lie against one another where they meet, so that one segment can transmit circumferential force to the other; the form of those surfaces and the extent of their engagement are shown schematically. Those surfaces in contact are the complementary coupling faces at the latch end 28.

## Numerals shown on this figure

- 10 first frame half: target well inside the hatched band between the groove of the upper body and its left edge.
- 12 second frame half: target well inside the hatched band between the groove of the lower body and its left edge.
- 18 annular guide: target the groove wall in the upper body on the radially inner side of the block that lies in the groove.
- 22 first cam ring segment: target the hatching inside the block in the upper body, away from the parting line.
- 24 second cam ring segment: target the hatching inside the block in the lower body, away from the parting line.
- 28 complementary coupling faces at the latch end: target the horizontal contact between the end faces of the two blocks at the parting line.
