# FIG. 2

View: enlarged longitudinal sectional view of the metering portion of FIG. 1, axis horizontal, with the piston in the fill position. All line work lies well inside the drawing area.

Geometry:

- The metering sleeve is cut lengthwise on its axis and appears as upper and lower hatched sleeve bands running across the view. The open space between them is the cylindrical dose chamber.
- The housing wall appears in section as hatched housing bands above and below the sleeve bands, spaced outward so that a clear gap runs above and below the sleeve.
- In the left portion of the chamber, an upstream sealing land is drawn as a hatched block whose upper and lower edges meet the bore walls, a downstream sealing land as a second such block to its right, and a waist joining them as a hatched bar of smaller height on the axis, with open space between the waist and each bore wall.
- A plain shaft on the axis extends leftward from the upstream sealing land and is broken off at a break line within the view.
- A radial opening passes through the upper sleeve band between the two sealing lands, its inner mouth opening into the space above the waist: the concentrate port. A short stub rises from it, broken off at a conventional break line, shown schematically as the connection to a concentrate source.
- A second opening passes through the lower sleeve band at the downstream sealing land, its inner mouth covered by that land and its outer mouth broken off at a break line within that band, so that what it opens into is not indicated: the carrier-liquid admission port, right of the concentrate port.
- A passage leads from the dose chamber into the upper sleeve band and is broken off at a break line within the sleeve band, so that what it opens into is not indicated, with a plain closure element across it, shown schematically: the one-way outlet valve.
- The two radial rinse openings shown in FIG. 3 are omitted from this view for clarity.
- The band in which each port and passage is drawn is a section-plane convention only; no circumferential relationship between them is intended.

Numerals on this sheet: 18, 20, 22, 24, 30, 32, 34, 38.

Targets:

- 18: the hatched lower sleeve band.
- 20: the open space of the dose chamber above the waist.
- 22: interior of the opening in the upper sleeve band above the waist.
- 24: interior of the opening at the downstream sealing land.
- 30: hatching of the left sealing land block.
- 32: hatching of the right sealing land block.
- 34: hatching of the narrower bar joining the two blocks.
- 38: the plain closure element in the passage.
