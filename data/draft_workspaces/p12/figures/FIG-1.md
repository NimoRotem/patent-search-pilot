# FIG. 1

View: longitudinal sectional view of the inline liquid metering valve, axis horizontal. All line work lies well inside the drawing area.

Geometry:

- The housing is an elongate cylinder cut lengthwise on its axis, appearing as upper and lower hatched bands joined at the right by a hatched end wall, the interior between them open.
- The left end of the housing is closed by a threaded end cap, a short hatched ring seated in the left mouth, its interface with the housing bore drawn as a shallow saw-tooth indicating a thread. A plain shaft extends leftward from the body out of the sleeve bore and is broken off at a break line.
- A short tubular stub projects leftward from the housing at its left end and opens into the interior: the inlet, shown schematically. A second such stub projects rightward from the end wall: the outlet.
- The metering sleeve lies inside the housing on the same axis as upper and lower hatched sleeve bands spaced inward from the housing bands, so that a clear gap is visible above and below it.
- The sleeve, the body in its bore and the parts they carry are drawn as one removable unit, the sleeve standing in the left mouth and retained there by the end cap ring: the cartridge.
- In the left half of the sleeve bore is a body shown schematically as two hatched enlarged portions joined by a narrower middle portion, the shaft running leftward.
- An unnumbered ring surrounds the sleeve over the left portion of its length. No relationship between this ring and the gap between the sleeve and the housing is indicated.
- An unhatched panel of simple outline, shown schematically, is set into the upper housing band above that ring: the transparent window. On the ring beneath it a small unnumbered patch shows.
- Where the inlet stub is drawn within the left end is a convention only; no radial position of, or relationship between, the inlet, the end cap ring and the shaft is intended. The axial end of the housing at which the end cap ring and the mouth receiving the removable unit are drawn is likewise a convention only, and no relationship between the end cap and either line connection is intended.

Numerals on this sheet: 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, 52, 64, 66.

Targets:

- 10: the assembly as a whole, by an arrowed lead line at the housing's outer contour.
- 12: the hatched upper housing band.
- 14: the wall of the stub at the left end.
- 16: the wall of the stub at the right end.
- 18: the hatched upper sleeve band.
- 52: the interior of the unhatched panel.
- 64: the removable unit as a whole, by an arrowed lead line stopping in open space just outside its outer boundary, clear of the sleeve wall bands that carry 18.
- 66: the hatched ring in the left mouth.
