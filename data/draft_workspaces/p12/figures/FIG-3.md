# FIG. 3

View: enlarged longitudinal section of the metering portion of FIG. 1 during the return stroke, axis horizontal, with the flush poppet held open. All line work lies well inside the drawing area.

Geometry:

- The housing wall appears in section as upper and lower hatched housing bands, the sleeve wall inside as upper and lower hatched sleeve bands spaced inward; between them is the bore.
- The clear gap between the sleeve and housing bands is the annular rinse channel, supplied with carrier liquid from the inlet end.
- In the left half of the bore, the piston is drawn as two hatched enlarged portions joined by a narrower middle portion, with a plain shaft extending leftward past the sleeve's left end and a short pin on it.
- Thin flexible projections extend from the left portion to the bore walls: the flexible lip, shown without flare direction.
- An opening through the upper sleeve band, its inner mouth clear of the sealing lands so that the rinse opening beneath it discharges across that mouth, is the concentrate port, with a short stub rising from it, broken off. The waist is not in register with the opening.
- A passage leads from the bore into the upper sleeve band and is broken off at a break line within that band, so that what it opens into is not indicated, with a plain closure element across it: the one-way outlet valve, unnumbered.
- Adjacent the left end of the sleeve, a plain closure element shown schematically is the flush poppet, held open by the pin and standing between the bore and a plain passage that runs from it into the upper sleeve band toward the outlet end and is broken off at a break line within that band, so that what it opens into is not indicated: the flush bypass.
- Two openings through the lower sleeve band connect the rinse channel to the bore: one adjacent the concentrate port and one adjacent the outlet-valve passage, each clear of the sealing lands.
- A further opening through the lower sleeve band, covered by the piston's right portion, its outer mouth broken off within that band: the carrier-liquid admission port, unnumbered.
- Which sleeve band each port and passage is drawn in is a section-plane convention only; no circumferential relationship between them is intended.

Numerals on this sheet: 22, 26, 28, 44, 56, 58, 60, 62.

Targets:

- 22: interior of the leftmost upper opening.
- 26: interior of the bypass passage.
- 28: the piston as a whole, by an arrowed lead line at its lower outline.
- 44: the short pin on the shaft.
- 56: the flexible projection at the upper bore wall.
- 58: the plain closure element held open by the pin.
- 60: the clear gap between the lower housing and sleeve bands.
- 62: the leftmost lower opening.
