# FIG. 4

View: fragmentary side view of the index collar and the parts that set and retain the dose, collar axis horizontal. All line work lies well inside the drawing area.

Geometry:

- The index collar is drawn as a cylindrical ring seen from the side: a long horizontal body, its right end drawn with a shallow curved edge. An unnumbered tube enters that end and is broken off at a break line within the collar, so that no axial relationship between the tube's end and the collar is indicated.
- A helical cam groove is drawn as a narrow band that wraps around the collar body, its outline in broken line throughout, shown schematically; the surface of the collar on which the groove is formed, and its hand and pitch, are not indicated.
- A piston rod is drawn as a plain horizontal shaft entering the view from the left on the collar axis, its length within the collar drawn as a hidden outline in broken line and broken off at a break line within the collar, so that no termination of the rod is indicated. A short follower pin projects radially from the shaft, drawn in broken line, its outer end within the narrow band of the groove.
- One end of the collar is drawn with its end face divided into three faces at three different axial positions, shown schematically: the three axial stop faces. Which end of the collar carries the faces, their order about the collar, and the part that abuts them, are not indicated.
- Three detent recesses lie at one station along the collar's length, at three angular positions about it, shown schematically as plain recesses: one broken into the outline and the other two in broken line. The surface of the collar on which the recesses are formed, their depth and profile, and the direction from which the detent element enters them are not indicated.
- Adjacent the collar, a spring-biased detent is shown as a plain detent element engaging that recess, with a spring behind it broken off; no contact relationship beyond the engagement itself is indicated.
- Three dose setting numerals lie at one station along the collar's length, at three angular positions about it, drawn as small plain patches, the middle one full on the visible surface; shown schematically, they carry no drawing text.

Numerals on this sheet: 36, 40, 42, 44, 46, 48, 50, 54.

Targets:

- 36: the shaft entering the view from the left.
- 40: the collar body, clear of the groove and recesses.
- 42: the interior of the groove band.
- 44: the short pin projecting from the shaft.
- 46: one of the three faces at the divided end.
- 48: the plain detent element engaging the recess.
- 50: the recess broken into the outline.
- 54: the patch full on the visible surface.
