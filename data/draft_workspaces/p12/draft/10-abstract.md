An inline liquid metering valve has a housing with an inlet and an outlet and a metering sleeve defining a dose chamber, a concentrate port, a carrier-liquid admission port and a flush bypass opening downstream of the dose chamber. A piston sliding in the dose chamber has two sealing lands joined by a reduced-diameter waist. In a fill position the waist connects the concentrate port to the dose chamber while the lands isolate the carrier-liquid admission port. Movement toward a discharge position closes the concentrate port before the downstream land uncovers the carrier-liquid admission port, so carrier liquid passes behind the measured volume and expels it through a one-way outlet valve. On the return stroke a follower pin on the piston rod opens a normally closed flush poppet, rinsing the wetted surfaces through an annular rinse channel and closing before the concentrate port is reconnected.
