An inline liquid metering valve delivers a repeatable measured volume of concentrate into a flowing carrier liquid and rinses the surfaces that contacted the concentrate after each dose, without any command additional to the stroke that delivers the dose.

The valve has a housing with an inlet and an outlet and a metering sleeve fixed within the housing. The metering sleeve defines a dose chamber, a concentrate port opening into the dose chamber, a carrier-liquid admission port opening into the dose chamber, and a flush bypass that opens downstream of the dose chamber. A piston slides axially in the dose chamber between a fill position and a discharge position. The piston has an upstream sealing land and a downstream sealing land axially spaced from one another and joined by a reduced-diameter waist.

Separation of the two liquids is enforced by the axial order of the land faces and the port edges rather than by the sequence in which any external command arrives. In the fill position, the waist connects the concentrate port to the dose chamber while the upstream and downstream sealing lands isolate the carrier-liquid admission port from the concentrate port. Movement of the piston toward the discharge position closes the concentrate port before the downstream sealing land uncovers the carrier-liquid admission port. Carrier liquid then enters behind the measured volume of concentrate and expels that volume through a one-way outlet valve toward the outlet. Because one land face has passed the concentrate port before the other land face releases the carrier-liquid admission port, no position of the piston places the concentrate source in communication with the carrier-liquid path.

The rinse is carried out by the return of the same piston. An annular rinse channel lies between an outer surface of the metering sleeve and an inner surface of the housing, and at least one rinse opening leads from that channel through the metering sleeve into the dose chamber. A follower pin carried by the piston rod opens a normally closed flush poppet connected to the flush bypass during the return stroke, so that carrier liquid sweeps the annular rinse channel, issues from the rinse opening across the surfaces wetted by the concentrate, and leaves through the flush bypass to the outlet. The flush poppet closes before the waist reconnects the concentrate port to the dose chamber, so the rinse is complete, and the rinse liquid is on its way to the outlet, before any further concentrate can enter. The rinse cannot be omitted, mistimed or deferred, because the element that opens it is carried by the piston rod itself.

In described embodiments, a flexible lip on the upstream sealing land wipes the wall of the dose chamber before the flush poppet opens. Two radial rinse openings are directed across the concentrate port and across the one-way outlet valve.

Dose selection is mechanical and self-indexing. An index collar surrounds the metering sleeve and has a helical cam groove engaged by the follower pin, so that each full stroke of the piston turns the index collar by one increment. Three selectable axial stop faces on the index collar limit the travel of the piston and thereby set three dose volumes, a spring-biased detent engaging one of three recesses retains the selected setting, and a transparent window in the housing shows one of three dose setting numerals carried by the index collar, so that the setting is visible without electronics.

A replaceable cartridge contains the piston, the metering sleeve, the one-way outlet valve and the flush poppet, and is retained by a threaded end cap. Removing the threaded end cap withdraws the cartridge without disturbing the connections at the inlet and the outlet.

A corresponding method admits concentrate to the dose chamber, closes the concentrate port before uncovering the carrier-liquid admission port, expels the measured volume through the one-way outlet valve, and then opens the flush poppet with the follower pin during the return of the piston and closes it again before the concentrate port is reconnected.
