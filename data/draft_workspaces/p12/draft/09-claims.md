1. An inline liquid metering valve, comprising:

a housing having an inlet and an outlet;

a metering sleeve fixed within the housing, the metering sleeve defining a dose chamber, a concentrate port opening into the dose chamber, a carrier-liquid admission port opening into the dose chamber, and a flush bypass that opens downstream of the dose chamber;

an annular rinse channel between an outer surface of the metering sleeve and an inner surface of the housing, and at least one rinse opening through the metering sleeve connecting the annular rinse channel to the dose chamber;

a piston axially slidable in the dose chamber between a fill position and a discharge position, the piston having an upstream sealing land and a downstream sealing land axially spaced from one another and joined by a reduced-diameter waist;

a piston rod extending from the piston and carrying a follower pin;

a one-way outlet valve arranged to pass liquid from the dose chamber toward the outlet; and

a normally closed flush poppet connected to the flush bypass;

wherein in the fill position the waist connects the concentrate port to the dose chamber and the upstream sealing land and the downstream sealing land isolate the carrier-liquid admission port from the concentrate port;

wherein movement of the piston toward the discharge position closes the concentrate port before the downstream sealing land uncovers the carrier-liquid admission port, whereby carrier liquid entering through the carrier-liquid admission port passes behind a measured volume of concentrate in the dose chamber and expels the measured volume through the one-way outlet valve toward the outlet; and

wherein the follower pin opens the flush poppet during a return stroke of the piston toward the fill position, whereby carrier liquid flows through the annular rinse channel, through the at least one rinse opening and through the flush bypass to the outlet, the flush poppet closing before the waist reconnects the concentrate port to the dose chamber.

2. The inline liquid metering valve of claim 1, wherein the at least one rinse opening comprises two radial rinse openings through the metering sleeve, one of the two radial rinse openings being directed across the concentrate port and the other of the two radial rinse openings being directed across the one-way outlet valve.

3. The inline liquid metering valve of claim 1, further comprising a flexible lip carried on the upstream sealing land and bearing on a wall of the dose chamber, the flexible lip wiping the wall of the dose chamber during the return stroke before the follower pin opens the flush poppet.

4. The inline liquid metering valve of claim 1, wherein the waist is out of register with the concentrate port, so that the waist does not connect the concentrate port to the dose chamber, throughout an interval in which the annular rinse channel communicates with the flush bypass.

5. The inline liquid metering valve of claim 1, wherein faces of the upstream sealing land and of the downstream sealing land and edges of the concentrate port and of the carrier-liquid admission port are axially positioned such that the concentrate port is closed at every position of the piston at which the carrier-liquid admission port is open.

6. The inline liquid metering valve of claim 1, further comprising an index collar surrounding the metering sleeve and having a helical cam groove engaged by the follower pin, the index collar being turned by one increment by each full stroke of the piston.

7. The inline liquid metering valve of claim 6, further comprising three selectable axial stop faces carried by the index collar at different axial positions, each of the three selectable axial stop faces, when selected, limiting travel of the piston to set a respective dose volume.

8. The inline liquid metering valve of claim 7, further comprising a spring-biased detent and three detent recesses, the spring-biased detent engaging one of the three detent recesses to retain a selected setting of the index collar.

9. 10. The inline liquid metering valve of claim 1, further comprising a cartridge containing the piston, the metering sleeve, the one-way outlet valve and the flush poppet, the cartridge being removable from the housing as a unit.

11. The inline liquid metering valve of claim 10, further comprising a threaded end cap retaining the cartridge in the housing, the threaded end cap being removable to withdraw the cartridge from the housing without disturbing connections at the inlet and at the outlet.

12. The inline liquid metering valve of claim 1, wherein the housing is cylindrical, the inlet being at one axial end of the housing and the outlet being at an opposite axial end of the housing.

13. A method of dosing a concentrate into a flowing carrier liquid, comprising:

flowing carrier liquid through a housing from an inlet to an outlet;

with a piston in a fill position in a dose chamber of a metering sleeve fixed within the housing, admitting concentrate through a concentrate port and past a reduced-diameter waist of the piston into the dose chamber to form a measured volume, an upstream sealing land and a downstream sealing land of the piston isolating a carrier-liquid admission port from the concentrate port;

advancing the piston toward a discharge position so as to close the concentrate port and thereafter to uncover the carrier-liquid admission port with the downstream sealing land;

admitting carrier liquid through the carrier-liquid admission port behind the measured volume and expelling the measured volume through a one-way outlet valve toward the outlet;

returning the piston toward the fill position;

during the returning, opening a normally closed flush poppet with a follower pin carried by a piston rod of the piston, and flowing carrier liquid through an annular rinse channel between an outer surface of the metering sleeve and an inner surface of the housing, through at least one rinse opening in the metering sleeve and through a flush bypass that opens downstream of the dose chamber to the outlet; and

closing the flush poppet before the waist reconnects the concentrate port to the dose chamber.

14. The method of claim 13, further comprising wiping a wall of the dose chamber with a flexible lip carried on the upstream sealing land during the returning and before the opening of the flush poppet.

15. The method of claim 13, wherein the flowing of carrier liquid through the at least one rinse opening comprises directing carrier liquid from one of two radial rinse openings across the concentrate port and from the other of the two radial rinse openings across the one-way outlet valve.

16. The method of claim 13, wherein the waist remains out of register with the concentrate port, so that the waist does not connect the concentrate port to the dose chamber, throughout an interval in which the annular rinse channel communicates with the flush bypass.

17. The method of claim 13, further comprising turning an index collar surrounding the metering sleeve by one increment for each full stroke of the piston by way of the follower pin engaged in a helical cam groove of the index collar.

18. The method of claim 17, further comprising limiting travel of the piston with a selected one of three axial stop faces carried by the index collar, thereby setting the measured volume.

19. The method of claim 18, further comprising retaining the selected one of the three axial stop faces with a spring-biased detent engaging one of three detent recesses, and displaying, through a transparent window in the housing, one of three dose setting numerals carried by the index collar.

20. The method of claim 13, further comprising removing a threaded end cap from the housing and withdrawing from the housing, as a unit, a cartridge containing the piston, the metering sleeve, the one-way outlet valve and the flush poppet, while the inlet and the outlet remain connected.
