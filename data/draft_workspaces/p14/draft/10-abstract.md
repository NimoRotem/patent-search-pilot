A reusable passive cold-chain carrier has an insulated outer shell, a removable product tray, a removable thermal module, and an insulated lid. Tray feet leave a return passage beneath the tray, whose side walls are spaced inward from the shell side walls to form two side supply passages. The module is a rigid spacer frame holding two opposed phase-change cassettes and having a central inlet opening, two downward guide ducts, and peripheral outlet openings. As the lid closes, a depending indexing rib enters a mating channel in the frame and shifts the module into a registered position in which the outlet openings overlap the side supply passages. Air circulates without a powered air mover, rising from the product region into the inlet opening, descending the guide ducts into the side supply passages, and returning through tray perforations and the return passage.
