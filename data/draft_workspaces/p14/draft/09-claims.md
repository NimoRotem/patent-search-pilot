1. A passive cold-chain carrier, comprising:

an insulated outer shell having a shell floor, shell side walls rising from the shell floor, and an upper edge at a top of the shell side walls;

a removable product tray within the insulated outer shell, the removable product tray having tray side walls, tray feet, and perforations therethrough, the tray feet suspending the removable product tray above the shell floor so that a lower return passage remains under the removable product tray, and the tray side walls being spaced inward from the shell side walls to form two side supply passages;

a removable thermal module comprising a rigid spacer frame holding two opposed phase-change cassettes, the rigid spacer frame resting above the removable product tray and including a central inlet opening, two downward guide ducts beside the phase-change cassettes, and peripheral outlet openings; and

an insulated lid above the removable thermal module and carrying a depending indexing rib, the rigid spacer frame having a mating channel that receives the depending indexing rib as the insulated lid closes, the depending indexing rib being configured to shift the removable thermal module, as the insulated lid closes, into a registered position in which the peripheral outlet openings overlap the side supply passages;

wherein the carrier has no fan, pump, or powered air mover, and wherein, in the registered position, the central inlet opening, faces of the phase-change cassettes, the downward guide ducts, the peripheral outlet openings, the side supply passages, the perforations, and the lower return passage define a circulation path that is closed on itself and along which air rising from a product region within the removable product tray returns to the product region.

2. The passive cold-chain carrier of claim 1, further comprising a compressible lid gasket carried by the insulated lid, wherein the depending indexing rib engages the mating channel and shifts the removable thermal module into the registered position, and continued closing of the insulated lid thereafter compresses the compressible lid gasket against the upper edge of the insulated outer shell.

3. The passive cold-chain carrier of claim 1, wherein the rigid spacer frame has resilient feet and the insulated outer shell has ledges, and closing of the insulated lid presses the resilient feet against the ledges so that the removable thermal module cannot lift away from the removable product tray during transport.

4. The passive cold-chain carrier of claim 1, wherein the two phase-change cassettes are held on opposite sides of the central inlet opening with faces of the phase-change cassettes mutually opposed, and each downward guide duct extends downward beside one of the phase-change cassettes and opens at a lower end of that downward guide duct.

5. The passive cold-chain carrier of claim 1, wherein the removable thermal module lifts out of the insulated outer shell as one piece with both phase-change cassettes, and the removable product tray thereafter lifts out of the insulated outer shell.

6. The passive cold-chain carrier of claim 1, further comprising a shallow condensate channel extending around the removable product tray, wherein condensate discharge openings at lower ends of the downward guide ducts discharge condensate into the shallow condensate channel.

7. The passive cold-chain carrier of claim 6, further comprising a removable absorbent wick lying in the shallow condensate channel.

8. The passive cold-chain carrier of claim 7, wherein the removable absorbent wick has a pull tab that remains accessible when the removable product tray is removed, so that the removable absorbent wick is replaced without opening a phase-change cassette.

9. The passive cold-chain carrier of claim 1, wherein the insulated outer shell includes a mechanical exposure indicator visible from outside, the mechanical exposure indicator comprising an indicator stem thermally coupled to one phase-change cassette, a temperature-responsive latch retaining the indicator stem, and a spring that moves the indicator stem to a latched exposed position when that phase-change cassette softens enough to release the temperature-responsive latch.

10. The passive cold-chain carrier of claim 9, wherein the indicator stem remains in the latched exposed position after that phase-change cassette cools again, and is reset manually only after the carrier is opened.

11. The passive cold-chain carrier of claim 9, wherein the mechanical exposure indicator does not control airflow, the circulation path being the same whether the indicator stem is retained by the temperature-responsive latch or has moved to the latched exposed position.

12. A method of passively conditioning a payload, comprising:

fitting two conditioned phase-change cassettes to a rigid spacer frame in mutually opposed positions to form a removable thermal module, the rigid spacer frame holding the two opposed phase-change cassettes and including a central inlet opening, two downward guide ducts beside the phase-change cassettes, and peripheral outlet openings;

placing products in a removable product tray;

placing the removable product tray in an insulated outer shell on tray feet that suspend the removable product tray above a shell floor of the insulated outer shell so that a lower return passage remains under the removable product tray, tray side walls of the removable product tray being spaced inward from shell side walls of the insulated outer shell to form two side supply passages;

placing the removable thermal module above the removable product tray;

closing an insulated lid onto the insulated outer shell so that a depending indexing rib carried by the insulated lid enters a mating channel of the rigid spacer frame and shifts the removable thermal module into a registered position in which the peripheral outlet openings overlap the side supply passages; and

thereafter circulating air passively, without a fan, pump, or powered air mover, from a product region within the removable product tray into the central inlet opening, along faces of the phase-change cassettes, downward through the downward guide ducts, through the peripheral outlet openings into the side supply passages, through perforations in the removable product tray, and back to the product region through the lower return passage.

13. The method of claim 12, wherein closing the insulated lid registers the removable thermal module, and continued closing thereafter compresses a compressible lid gasket carried by the insulated lid against an upper edge of the insulated outer shell.

14. The method of claim 12, wherein closing the insulated lid presses resilient feet of the rigid spacer frame against ledges of the insulated outer shell so that the removable thermal module cannot lift away from the removable product tray during transport.

15. The method of claim 12, further comprising, at a destination, opening the insulated lid, lifting the removable thermal module out of the insulated outer shell as one piece with both phase-change cassettes, and thereafter lifting the removable product tray out of the insulated outer shell.

16. A method of servicing a passive cold-chain carrier between uses, comprising:

opening an insulated lid of the carrier and withdrawing a depending indexing rib carried by the insulated lid from a mating channel of a rigid spacer frame of a removable thermal module, the rigid spacer frame holding two opposed phase-change cassettes and having two downward guide ducts beside the phase-change cassettes;

lifting the removable thermal module out of an insulated outer shell of the carrier;

lifting a removable product tray out of the insulated outer shell, a pull tab of a removable absorbent wick lying in a shallow condensate channel that extends around the removable product tray remaining accessible when the removable product tray is removed, the shallow condensate channel receiving condensate from condensate discharge openings at lower ends of the downward guide ducts;

withdrawing the removable absorbent wick from the shallow condensate channel by the pull tab and placing a replacement absorbent wick in the shallow condensate channel, without opening a phase-change cassette; and

returning the removable product tray and the removable thermal module to the insulated outer shell and closing the insulated lid so that the depending indexing rib enters the mating channel and shifts the removable thermal module into a registered position in which peripheral outlet openings of the rigid spacer frame overlap side supply passages formed between tray side walls of the removable product tray and shell side walls of the insulated outer shell.

17. The method of claim 16, further comprising resetting by hand a mechanical exposure indicator of the insulated outer shell from a latched exposed position to which a spring moved an indicator stem of the mechanical exposure indicator when a phase-change cassette thermally coupled to the indicator stem softened enough to release a temperature-responsive latch.
