Here is my invention.

A reusable passive cold-chain carrier has an insulated outer shell, a removable product tray, a removable thermal module, and an insulated lid. The product tray is suspended above the shell floor by feet so that a lower return passage remains under the tray. The tray side walls are spaced inward from the shell side walls to form two side supply passages.

The thermal module is a rigid spacer frame holding two opposed phase-change cassettes. The spacer frame rests above the product tray and below the lid. It includes a central inlet opening, two downward guide ducts beside the cassettes, and peripheral outlet openings aligned with the side supply passages. Warm air rising from the product region enters the central inlet, divides along faces of the cassettes, descends through the guide ducts, enters the side supply passages, passes through perforations in the product tray, and returns through the lower return passage. The carrier is passive. It has no fan, pump, or powered air mover.

The lid carries a depending indexing rib. As the lid closes, the rib enters a mating channel in the spacer frame and shifts the thermal module into a registered position in which the peripheral outlet openings overlap the side supply passages. A compressible lid gasket seals against the upper edge of the shell after registration. The same closing movement presses resilient feet of the spacer frame against ledges of the shell so that the module cannot lift away from the tray during transport.

A removable absorbent wick lies in a shallow condensate channel around the product tray. Openings at the lower ends of the guide ducts discharge condensate into the channel. A pull tab on the wick remains accessible when the product tray is removed, allowing the wick to be replaced without opening a phase-change cassette.
