# FIG. 6

Enlarged schematic vertical section of the lower right region of the closed carrier indicated by the right-hand lower broken circle in FIG. 2, black line work on white, patent drawing style. Sectioned solids carry uniform diagonal hatching; open spaces are blank. The outside of the carrier is toward the right, the inside toward the left. Keep all line work well inside the drawing area, clear of the sheet edges.

- Shell side walls 14: an upright hatched slab of substantial thickness at the right-hand edge of the drawing area, running from the top of the view and joined at its lower end to the shell floor 12.
- Shell floor 12: a horizontal hatched slab of substantial thickness along the bottom of the view, running leftward from the shell side wall 14 to the inboard edge of the view.
- Shallow condensate channel 70: a shallow, upwardly open trough, wider than it is deep, formed in the upper surface of the shell floor 12 immediately inboard of the shell side wall 14. The numeral 70 is led to a point well inside the trough.
- Removable absorbent wick 72: an elongate body lying in the trough and filling its lower part, hatched distinctly from the surrounding solids.
- Pull tab 74: a small tab on the wick 72 rising out of the open top of the trough, shown schematically.
- Removable product tray 20: shown as one hatched L-shaped profile made up of the tray side wall 24 and a continuous horizontal bottom panel. The numeral 20 is led to the horizontal bottom panel of this L-shaped profile.
- Tray side walls 24: the upright limb of the L-shaped profile, a hatched panel of modest thickness spaced inboard from the shell side wall 14. The numeral 24 is led to the upright limb of the L-shaped profile.
- Tray bottom panel: the horizontal limb of that profile, unnumbered, running leftward from the tray side wall 24 to the inboard edge of the view, its underside spaced above the shell floor 12 with blank open space between.
- Tray feet 22: one short narrow upright hatched pillar standing on the shell floor 12 and supporting the underside of the tray bottom panel, positioned to the left of the trough and clear of it.

- The wick 72 lies in the trough of the channel 70 along its length.
