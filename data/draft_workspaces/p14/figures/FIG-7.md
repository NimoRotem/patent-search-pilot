# FIG. 7

Enlarged schematic vertical section of the wall region of the closed carrier indicated by the left-hand broken circle in FIG. 2, taken at the mechanical exposure indicator, black line work on white, patent drawing style. Sectioned solids carry uniform diagonal hatching; open spaces are blank. The outside of the carrier is toward the left, the inside toward the right. No lettering, words, or characters of any kind are to be drawn. The only text on the sheet is the figure caption and the reference numerals listed below. Keep all line work well inside the drawing area.

- Shell side walls 14: an upright hatched slab of substantial thickness running the full height of the view, its outer surface facing left, the hatching being interrupted where the indicator stem 82 passes through the slab.
- Rigid spacer frame 42: a hatched portion of the frame body inboard of the shell side wall 14, defining a boundary of the downward guide duct 48.
- Downward guide ducts 48: a blank open upright passage inboard of a web of the frame 42.
- Mechanical exposure indicator 80: the indicator assembly at the outer surface of the shell side wall 14, comprising the stem 82, the latch 84 and the spring 86, shown schematically within an unnumbered plain outline, the numeral 80 being led to the assembly as a whole.
- Indicator stem 82: an elongate bar extending inward from the indicator 80 through the shell side wall 14 toward the phase-change cassette 44, drawn with its own outline and its own hatching pattern distinct from that of the wall 14 and the frame 42.
- Temperature-responsive latch 84: shown schematically, retaining the indicator stem 82 against outward movement.
- Spring 86: shown schematically as a compression spring. It is drawn as a simple coil of three or four open, rounded, oval turns. It must not be drawn as a zigzag or as sharp peaks that could be mistaken for lettering.
- Phase-change cassettes 44: a slab-like body at the right of the view, drawn with its own outline and its own hatching pattern, the inner end of the stem 82 reaching toward it.

- An unnumbered schematic thermal coupling connects the inner end of the indicator stem 82 to the phase-change cassette 44 at the right of the view.
- The latch 84 holds the stem 82 against the outward action of the spring 86.
