# FIG. 2

Schematic vertical section on line 2-2 of FIG. 1. Sectioned solids are hatched, open spaces blank. Keep all line work well inside the drawing area.

- Shell floor 12: a horizontal hatched slab at the bottom of the view.
- Shell side walls 14: two upright hatched slabs forming a U-shaped section with the shell floor 12. Each side wall 14 has an unnumbered inward projection.
- Removable product tray 20: an upwardly open hatched pan inside the U-section, with shorter side panels. The tray 20 is shown supported on tray feet 22 (not shown in this view) and is not in contact with the removable thermal module 40.
- Perforations 26: small gaps through the bottom of the tray 20.
- Lower return passage 28: the open space between the bottom of tray 20 and the shell floor 12.
- Side supply passages 30: two upright open spaces between a tray side wall and the adjacent shell side wall 14.
- Removable thermal module 40: a shallow hatched body located above the tray 20, supported on the inward projections of the shell side walls 14. The module is open downward at its center and holds two phase-change cassettes 44. A downward passage beside each cassette 44 meets a side supply passage 30 below through a peripheral gap. An unnumbered recess is in its upper surface.
- Insulated lid 60: a horizontal hatched slab closing the top of the view above the module 40. An unnumbered band lies between its underside and the top of each shell side wall 14. An unnumbered projection depends from its underside into the module's recess.
- Flow arrows: unnumbered arrows show a closed air circuit rising from the tray 20, into the module 40 central opening, along the cassettes 44, down into the side supply passages 30, through the perforations 26, and back via the lower return passage 28.
- Five unnumbered broken circles mark the regions enlarged in FIG. 3-7.
