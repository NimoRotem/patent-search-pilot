# FIG. 3

Enlarged schematic vertical section of the central upper region of the closed carrier indicated by the central broken circle in FIG. 2, on the same upright plane cutting from side to side, black line work on white, patent drawing style. Sectioned solids carry uniform diagonal hatching; open spaces are blank. Keep all line work well inside the drawing area, clear of the sheet edges and filing margins.

- Insulated lid 60: a horizontal hatched slab of substantial thickness spanning the top of the view.
- Rigid spacer frame 42: a hatched frame body of visible wall thickness below the lid 60, defining boundaries of the central inlet opening 46 and the downward guide ducts 48.
- Central inlet opening 46: the blank open region at the centre of the width of the frame 42, open downward at the underside of the frame 42 toward the tray and continuous upward with the blank open space between the two cassettes 44.
- Phase-change cassettes 44: two upright slab-like bodies held within the frame 42, one on each side of the opening 46, each with its own outline and hatching pattern distinct from that of the frame 42, facing one another across a blank open space directly above the opening 46.
- Downward guide ducts 48: two blank open upright passages within the frame 42, one beside each cassette 44, each open at its upper end to the open space into which air from the central inlet opening 46 divides along the faces of the cassettes 44, and open at its lower end at the bottom of the view.
- Mating channel 52: an upwardly open recess in an upper surface of the frame 42, shown schematically, configured to receive the depending indexing rib 62.
- Depending indexing rib 62: a downwardly projecting hatched member on the underside of the lid 60, shown schematically, aligned above the mating channel 52 and entering it.

- Air entering the opening 46 divides along the faces of the cassettes 44 and passes into the ducts 48, which lead it downward.
- The rib 62 is engaged in the channel 52 while the lid 60 lies above the frame 42.
