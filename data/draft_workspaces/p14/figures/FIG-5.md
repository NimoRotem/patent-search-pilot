# FIG. 5

Enlarged schematic vertical section of the region of the closed carrier indicated by the right-hand middle broken circle in FIG. 2, at the lower end of the right-hand downward guide duct where it meets the outer wall region of the carrier, on the same upright plane cutting from side to side, black line work on white, patent drawing style. Sectioned solids carry uniform diagonal hatching; open spaces are blank. The outside of the carrier is toward the right of the drawing area and the inside toward the left. Exactly three hatched solids appear in this view and no others: the shell side wall 14 at the right, the rigid spacer frame 42 in the upper part of the view, and the tray side wall 24 below and inboard of the frame 42; every other region of the view is blank open space. Keep all line work well inside the drawing area, clear of the sheet edges and filing margins.

- Shell side walls 14: an upright hatched slab of substantial thickness at the outboard side of the view, at the right-hand edge of the drawing area, running the full height of the view from top to bottom.
- Rigid spacer frame 42: a hatched frame body inboard of the shell side wall 14 in the upper part of the view, defining a boundary of the downward guide duct 48.
- Downward guide ducts 48: a blank open upright passage inboard of the outboard web of the frame 42, running downward to its lower end.
- Peripheral outlet openings 50: a gap at the periphery of the frame 42 at the lower end of the ducts 48, through which the ducts 48 communicate with the upper end of the side supply passages 30, the opening 50 overlapping those passages, shown schematically.
- Condensate discharge openings 56: an opening for condensate at the lower end of the downward guide ducts 48, drawn as a break in the outline of the frame 42 at the bottom of the duct, shown schematically.
- Side supply passages 30: a blank open upright space below the peripheral outlet openings 50, between the inner face of the shell side walls 14 and the tray side walls 24 inboard of it, open at its lower end at the bottom of the view.
- Tray side walls 24: a single upright hatched panel of modest thickness inboard of and spaced from the shell side walls 14. The panel begins below the frame 42 and runs downward to the bottom edge of the drawing area.

- The duct 48 communicates with the upper end of the side supply passage 30 through the peripheral outlet opening 50, the opening 50 overlapping the upper end of that passage so that the two are in open communication.
