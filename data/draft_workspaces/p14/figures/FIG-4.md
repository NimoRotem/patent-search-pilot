# FIG. 4

Enlarged schematic vertical section of the region of the closed carrier indicated by the right-hand upper broken circle in FIG. 2, where the insulated lid meets the top of the outer wall of the carrier and the right end of the rigid spacer frame is held down, on the same upright plane cutting from side to side, black line work on white, patent drawing style. Sectioned solids carry uniform diagonal hatching; open spaces are blank. Keep all line work well inside the drawing area, clear of the sheet edges and filing margins.

- Shell side walls 14: an upright hatched slab of substantial thickness at the outboard side of the view, running downward from the top of the wall to the bottom of the view, its upper end being the upper edge 16 of the insulated outer shell.
- Insulated lid 60: a horizontal hatched slab of substantial thickness spanning the top of the view above the upper edge 16.
- Compressible lid gasket 64: a band shown compressed between the underside of the insulated lid 60 and the upper edge 16, hatched distinctly from both slabs.
- Ledges 18: a ledge on the inward-facing surface of the shell side walls 14, shown schematically.
- Rigid spacer frame 42: a hatched frame body inboard of the shell side wall 14 and below the insulated lid 60, its outboard portion shown in this view, an unnumbered blank open passage running downward within it inboard of its outboard web.
- Resilient feet 54: a resilient foot 54 of the rigid spacer frame 42, in contact with and bearing down on the ledge 18.

- The insulated lid 60 lies above the frame 42 while the foot 54 sits on the ledge 18 and the gasket 64 is compressed against the upper edge 16.
