# FIG. 1

Exploded perspective view of a reusable passive cold-chain carrier, black line work on white, patent drawing style. The four bodies are separated vertically along a common upright axis, seen from slightly above one front corner so the top of each lower body is visible. Keep all line work well inside the drawing area, clear of the sheet edges and filing margins.

- Insulated outer shell 10: an open-topped rectangular box with visible wall thickness, shown schematically, its interior shown open, the ledges of the shell and the shallow condensate channel not being shown in this view. Its rim, the upper edge 16, is the band at the top of the wall thickness, running around the top of the box.
- Removable product tray 20: an open-topped rectangular pan, shown schematically, smaller in plan than the shell 10 and with lower walls, directly above the shell 10.
- Perforations 26: small through holes in the tray 20, shown schematically.
- Removable thermal module 40: a flat assembly floating above the tray 20, about the same outline in plan as the opening of the shell 10 so that it spans the tray 20 and the space at each side of it, drawn schematically as a shallow rectangular frame body of visible thickness, an unnumbered upwardly open recess being formed in its upper surface; the phase-change cassettes held within the frame body are not visible in this view.
- Insulated lid 60: a rectangular slab, shown schematically, floating above the module 40, matching the shell 10 in plan, shown in the same upright attitude as the other bodies.

A broken cutting-plane line crosses the lid 60, the module 40, the tray 20, and the shell 10 horizontally at the same position across the common upright axis, each segment running from a point clear of the left outer wall of that body to a point clear of its right outer wall, parallel to the front of the upper edge 16. Together the segments are line 2-2, the same section designation being repeated at each end of the line, and an arrow at each end points toward the rear, the direction in which the section of FIG. 2 is viewed. The section designation is drawing annotation, not a reference numeral.
