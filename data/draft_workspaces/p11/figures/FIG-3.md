# FIG. 3

View: enlarged schematic block diagram of the edge controller of FIG. 1, in plain line work. All line work lies well inside the drawing area, clear of the filing margins. No text is drawn by the geometry; numerals are applied afterward.

Geometry:

- A large plain rectangle stands at the centre of the drawing area and encloses the elements of the controller. This enclosing rectangle is the edge controller 100, shown schematically.
- A plain rectangle stands within the enclosing rectangle in its upper part. This rectangle is the nonvolatile memory 132, shown schematically.
- A short horizontal line lies well inside the upper part of the drawing area, above the enclosing rectangle. This line is the branch conductor 102. A small open rectangle straddles it, the line passing through it and continuing right. This rectangle is the branch current sensor 104, shown schematically. A line runs from that sensor down to the top side of the enclosing rectangle, joining it to the edge controller 100.
- A plain rectangle stands outside the enclosing rectangle at its left, joined by a horizontal line to the left side of the enclosing rectangle. This rectangle is the network interface 108, shown schematically, and a short line extends from its left side into the drawing area.
- A plain rectangle stands outside the enclosing rectangle at its lower left, joined to the lower left side of the enclosing rectangle by a short horizontal line. This rectangle is the local fault indicator 134, shown schematically.
- A plain rectangle stands outside the enclosing rectangle at its lower right, joined to the lower right side of the enclosing rectangle by a short horizontal line. This rectangle is the service input 136, shown schematically. A short line extends downward from it and terminates inside the drawing area.
- A line runs from the right side of the enclosing rectangle to a point well inside the right part of the drawing area. This line is the isolated local bus 106. Two short parallel vertical strokes cross it outside the enclosing rectangle, indicating isolation schematically.

Numeral endpoints: end each numeral leader well inside the body it identifies. For the edge controller 100, end the leader on the outline of the enclosing rectangle away from any line crossing it. For the isolated local bus 106, end the leader on that line at a point whose horizontal position lies to the right of the right side of the enclosing rectangle and clear of the two short parallel strokes crossing that line. End the leader for the branch current sensor 104 well inside that small open rectangle, and for the branch conductor 102 on that line clear of the small open rectangle straddling it.
