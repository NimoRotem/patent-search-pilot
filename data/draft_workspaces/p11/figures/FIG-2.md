# FIG. 2

View: enlarged schematic block diagram of the first connector station of FIG. 1, in plain line work. All line work lies well inside the drawing area, clear of the filing margins. No text is drawn by the geometry; numerals are applied afterward.

Geometry:

- A large plain rectangle encloses the station elements. This enclosing rectangle is the first connector station 110, shown schematically.
- A short horizontal line at the upper left of the drawing area, left of the enclosing rectangle, is the branch conductor 102. It enters through the upper left side of that rectangle and continues inside as the power path running right.
- Two small open rectangles straddle the power path in the left part of the enclosing rectangle, the path passing through each. The left one is the first contactor 120 and the right one is the first connector current sensor 122, both shown schematically.
- The power path ends at the left side of a plain rectangle in the right part of the enclosing rectangle, the first electric-vehicle connector 126, shown schematically, from whose right side a short line extends into the drawing area.
- A plain rectangle stands in the lower middle of the enclosing rectangle, below the power path, the first control-pilot interface 124, shown schematically. A line runs from its top side up to the lower side of the first electric-vehicle connector 126.
- A horizontal line lies below the enclosing rectangle and extends leftward past the left side of that rectangle, ending well inside the left part of the drawing area, so that a left portion of this line lies entirely to the left of the left side of the enclosing rectangle. It rises into that rectangle through its lower side and branches inside to the first control-pilot interface 124, to the first contactor 120, and to the first connector current sensor 122. This line is the isolated local bus 106.

The first contactor 120 and the first connector current sensor 122 lie in series in the same power path; their order is a drawing convention.

Numeral endpoints: end each numeral leader well inside the body it identifies. For the first connector station 110, end the leader on the outline of the enclosing rectangle away from any line crossing it. For the branch conductor 102, end the leader on the portion of that line lying to the left of the enclosing rectangle. For the isolated local bus 106, end the leader on that line at a point whose horizontal position lies to the left of the left side of the enclosing rectangle, outside that rectangle's horizontal span.
