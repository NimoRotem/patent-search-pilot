# FIG. 4

View: process flow diagram of the allocation interval and of the staged branch-protection sequence, in plain line work with no shading. All line work lies well inside the drawing area, clear of the filing margins. No text or lettering is drawn by the geometry; the numerals below are applied afterward. Each step is a plain rectangle, and the steps are joined by lines carrying a single arrowhead that shows the direction of flow.

Geometry:

- Four rectangles are stacked in a column in the left half of the drawing area. From the top downward these are the available-charging-current determination step 200, the minimum sustaining current assignment step 202, the deficit-based distribution step 204, and the limit transmission and connector current verification step 206. A line with a downward arrowhead joins each of these rectangles to the one below it.
- Three rectangles are stacked in a column in the right half of the drawing area. From the top downward these are the pilot reduction step 208, the ordered contactor shedding step 210, and the reclose permissive step 212. Lines with downward arrowheads join 208 to 210 and 210 to 212.
- A horizontal line runs from the right side of the limit transmission and connector current verification step 206 to the left side of the pilot reduction step 208, with an arrowhead at that left side.
- A rectangle stands in the lower right region of the drawing area, below and to the right of the ordered contactor shedding step 210. This rectangle is the welded-contactor isolation step 214. A line runs from the right side of the ordered contactor shedding step 210 to the top side of the welded-contactor isolation step 214, with an arrowhead at that top side.
- A further line leaves the left side of the ordered contactor shedding step 210, runs leftward clear of every rectangle, and joins the return line described below, so that the process may return to the allocation interval from the ordered contactor shedding step 210 without passing through the reclose permissive step 212.
- A return line leaves the bottom side of the reclose permissive step 212, runs downward and then to the left below both columns and clear of every rectangle, turns upward along the left part of the drawing area, and ends with an arrowhead at the left side of the available-charging-current determination step 200, so that the process returns to the allocation interval.

Numeral endpoints: end each numeral leader well inside the rectangle it identifies.
