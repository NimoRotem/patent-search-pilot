# FIG. 1

View: schematic block diagram of the charging control system, in plain line work. All line work lies well inside the drawing area, clear of the filing margins. No text is drawn by the geometry; numerals are applied afterward.

Geometry:

- A horizontal line spans the upper third of the drawing area. This line is the branch conductor 102.
- A small open rectangle straddles the branch conductor 102 near its left end, the line passing through it and continuing right. This rectangle is the branch current sensor 104, shown schematically.
- Three vertical drop lines descend from the branch conductor 102 to the right of the branch current sensor 104, each ending at the top edge of one of three plain rectangles standing side by side in the middle band of the drawing area.
- The leftmost of the three rectangles is the first connector station 110, the middle rectangle is the second connector station 112, and the rightmost rectangle is the non-charging load 114, all shown schematically.
- A larger plain rectangle stands in the lower left of the drawing area, below and left of the first connector station 110. This rectangle is the edge controller 100, shown schematically.
- A horizontal line runs from the right side of the edge controller 100 across the lower band of the drawing area, with two vertical branches rising from it to the bottom edges of the first connector station 110 and the second connector station 112. This line is the isolated local bus 106. Two short parallel strokes cross it between the edge controller 100 and the left branch, indicating isolation schematically.
- A line runs from the branch current sensor 104 downward and to the left, clear of the three rectangles, to the top edge of the edge controller 100, joining that sensor to the edge controller 100.
- A small plain rectangle stands outside the edge controller 100 at its left, joined to it by a short horizontal line. This rectangle is the network interface 108, shown schematically, and a short line extends from its left side into the drawing area.

Numeral endpoints: end each numeral leader well inside the body it identifies. For the branch conductor 102, end the leader on that line clear of any junction and of the small open rectangle straddling it. For the isolated local bus 106, end the leader on the horizontal portion of that line, clear of any junction and of the two short parallel strokes crossing it.
