# FIG. 2

A top plan view of the drilling jig.

The long, straight rail 10 is seen from above and extends from left to right, its first end and its second end both ending well inside the borders of the drawing area, so that no part of the view touches a border. A longitudinal slot 16 runs lengthwise along the rail 10 as a straight elongated opening, shown schematically.

Distance marks 18 are a row of short transverse graduation lines on the upper face of the rail 10, along one long side of the longitudinal slot 16.

The fence 30 is at the left end of the rail 10 and is shown schematically as a plain rectangular body crossing the rail 10 transverse to its length.

The first guide carriage 50 (nearer the fence 30) and the second guide carriage 70 (farther from the fence 30) are two separate bodies, shown schematically as plain rectangular bodies, resting on top of the rail 10. Each carriage overlaps and hides the part of the longitudinal slot 16 beneath it. The clamp knob 58 of the first guide carriage 50 and the clamp knob 78 of the second guide carriage 70 are shown.

A broken, offset cutting-plane line establishes the cross-sectional view of FIG. 5. This is line 5-5. The line crosses the first guide carriage 50 transverse to the length of the rail 10, beginning clear of one long side of the rail 10 and ending clear of the other long side. The line is stepped to pass through the axis of the drill bushing bore of that carriage and the axis of its clamp knob. Both ends of line 5-5 have viewing arrows pointing to the left, toward the fence 30. Both ends of line 5-5 have the repeated designation 5.

A second broken, offset cutting-plane line establishes the cross-sectional view of FIG. 8. This is line 8-8. The line crosses the second guide carriage 70 transverse to the length of the rail 10, beginning clear of one long side of the rail 10 and ending clear of the other long side. The line is stepped to pass through the axis of the drill bushing bore of that carriage and the axis of its clamp knob. Both ends of line 8-8 have viewing arrows pointing to the left, toward the fence 30. Both ends of line 8-8 have the repeated designation 8.

## Numerals shown on this figure

- 10: rail
- 16: longitudinal slot

- 30: fence
- 50: first guide carriage
- 58: clamp knob of the first guide carriage
- 70: second guide carriage
- 78: clamp knob of the second guide carriage
