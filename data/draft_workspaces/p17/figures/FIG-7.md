# FIG. 7

A fragmentary top plan view of the second end portion of the rail, resting on a workpiece, showing the centering features.

The workpiece 90 is a flat panel seen from above. It is shown as a fragment with ragged break lines on all four sides to indicate it is a fragment of a larger panel.

A fragmentary portion of the rail 10 lies on the workpiece 90. The rail's left end in the view is a ragged break line, indicating the rail continues. The rail's right end is its finished second end, which is located well short of the right side of the drawing area. The workpiece 90 is visible on all sides of the rail fragment.

The longitudinal slot 16 is shown schematically as a straight, elongated rectangle running lengthwise along the rail 10.

A transverse center index 24 is a straight stroke of visible thickness lying on the upper face of the rail 10 in the left part of the fragment, well away from the second end of the rail 10. It runs transverse to the length of the rail 10 and is shown schematically, no part of it being drawn across the opening of the longitudinal slot 16. A single leader from the numeral 24 points to the transverse center index 24.

A sight notch 26 is a shallow V-shaped notch formed in the second end of the rail 10 and aligned with the transverse center index 24, shown schematically.

A centerline 94 is a straight pencil line drawn on the workpiece 90. The rail 10 lies on top of the workpiece 90 and fully occludes the portion of the centerline 94 beneath it. The centerline 94 is therefore visible only on the parts of the workpiece 90 not covered by the rail, where it emerges from under the rail. The visible portions of the centerline 94 are aligned with the transverse center index 24 and with the sight notch 26.

## Numerals shown on this figure

- 10: rail
- 16: longitudinal slot
- 24: transverse center index
- 26: sight notch
- 90: workpiece
- 94: centerline
