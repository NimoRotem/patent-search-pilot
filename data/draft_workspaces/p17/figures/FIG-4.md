# FIG. 4

An elevation view of the drilling jig on a workpiece 90, with the spacer plate 40 installed.

The workpiece 90 is seen edgewise as a flat panel, its upper long side being the front face of the workpiece 90 and its straight left-hand side being the workpiece edge 92. The whole view lies well inside the drawing area. The view is taken looking along the length of the rail 10, from the second end toward the first end.

The rail 10 is seen end-on, resting by its broad, flat lower face 14 on the front face of the workpiece 90 and set inward from the workpiece edge 92.

The fence 30 is shown schematically as a plain body crossing the rail 10 transverse to its length and reaching from the rail 10 toward the workpiece edge 92. A downwardly projecting portion 32 of the fence 30 stands on the longitudinal side of the rail 10 that faces the workpiece edge 92 and extends below the level of the lower face 14.

The spacer plate 40 is a flat plate standing between the downwardly projecting portion 32 and the workpiece edge 92, with one face against the downwardly projecting portion 32 and the opposite face against the workpiece edge 92.

## Numerals shown on this figure

- 10: rail
- 14: lower face
- 30: fence
- 32: downwardly projecting portion
- 40: spacer plate
- 90: workpiece
- 92: workpiece edge
