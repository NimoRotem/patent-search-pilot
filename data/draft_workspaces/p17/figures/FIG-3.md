# FIG. 3

A fragmentary top plan view of the first and second guide carriages, showing the pointers.

The view shows a portion of the rail 10 with the distance marks 18 along the longitudinal slot. The first guide carriage 50 and the second guide carriage 70 are shown on the rail 10. The first pointer 62 on the first guide carriage 50 and the second pointer 82 on the second guide carriage 70 are shown aligned with the distance marks 18. The drill bushing 54 of the first guide carriage and the drill bushing 74 of the second guide carriage are indicated schematically.

## Numerals shown on this figure

- 10: rail
- 18: distance marks
- 50: first guide carriage
- 54: drill bushing of the first guide carriage
- 62: first pointer
- 70: second guide carriage
- 74: drill bushing of the second guide carriage
- 82: second pointer
