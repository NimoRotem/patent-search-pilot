# FIG. 8

A cross-sectional view taken on line 8-8 of FIG. 2.

The whole view lies well inside the drawing area, clear of the borders on every side. The surfaces cut by the section plane are shown with regular, evenly spaced section hatching. To distinguish adjacent components, their hatching is in different directions. The cut surface of the rail 10 is hatched with lines slanting in one direction. The cut surface of the second guide carriage 70, which is adjacent to the rail 10, is hatched with lines slanting in a different direction. The drill bushing 74 is a separate component carried by the second guide carriage 70, and its cut surface is hatched in a third direction, different from that of the second guide carriage 70.

The view shows the rail 10 in cross-section, with the longitudinal slot 16 passing through it. The rail has an upper face 12.

The second guide carriage 70 sits on the upper face 12 of the rail 10. A key 72 projects downward from the carriage 70 into the longitudinal slot 16.

The clamp knob 78 is above the carriage 70. A threaded shank extends from the knob, through the carriage 70 and the slot 16, and into a clamping shoe 80 located below the rail 10.

A cylindrical drill bushing 74 is carried by the carriage 70, its vertical bore passing completely through the carriage 70 and aligned with the slot 16.

## Numerals shown on this figure

- 10: rail
- 12: upper face
- 16: longitudinal slot
- 70: second guide carriage
- 72: key of the second guide carriage
- 74: drill bushing of the second guide carriage
- 78: clamp knob of the second guide carriage
- 80: clamping shoe of the second guide carriage
