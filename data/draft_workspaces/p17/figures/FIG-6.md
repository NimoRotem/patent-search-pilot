# FIG. 6

An exploded perspective view of the first end of the rail 10 and the reversible fence 30. The whole view lies well inside the drawing area, clear of the borders on every side.

The fence 30 has two holes 36 for receiving the two screws 34. The fence 30 is shown lifted off the rail. The fence 30 has a downwardly projecting portion 32 that lies on one longitudinal side of the rail 10 when the fence 30 is installed. Two screws 34 are shown aligned with the two holes 36 by which the fence 30 is secured to the first end of the rail 10.

## Numerals shown on this figure

- 10: rail
- 30: fence
- 32: downwardly projecting portion
- 34: screw
- 36: hole in fence
