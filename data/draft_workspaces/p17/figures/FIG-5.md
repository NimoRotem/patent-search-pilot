# FIG. 5

A cross-sectional view taken on line 5-5 of FIG. 2.

The whole view lies well inside the drawing area, clear of the borders on every side. The surfaces cut by the section plane are shown with regular, evenly spaced section hatching. The rail 10 and the first guide carriage 50 are adjacent parts in this view, and their cut surfaces are hatched in different directions to distinguish them.

The view shows the rail 10 in cross-section, with the longitudinal slot 16 passing through it. The rail has an upper face 12.

The first guide carriage 50 sits on the upper face 12 of the rail 10. A key 52 projects downward from the carriage 50 into the longitudinal slot 16.

The clamp knob 58 is above the carriage 50. A threaded shank extends from the knob, through the carriage 50 and the slot 16, and into a clamping shoe 60 located below the rail 10.

A cylindrical drill bushing 54 is carried by the carriage 50, its vertical bore passing completely through the carriage 50 and aligned with the slot 16.

## Numerals shown on this figure

- 10: rail
- 12: upper face
- 16: longitudinal slot
- 50: first guide carriage
- 52: key of the first guide carriage
- 54: drill bushing of the first guide carriage
- 58: clamp knob of the first guide carriage
- 60: clamping shoe of the first guide carriage
