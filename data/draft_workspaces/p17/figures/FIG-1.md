# FIG. 1

A perspective view of a drilling jig on a workpiece 90.

The workpiece 90 is a flat panel, and one straight side of the panel is the workpiece edge 92.

A long straight rail 10 rests on the front face of the workpiece 90, spaced inward from the workpiece edge 92, well inside the drawing area. A fence 30 is attached to the left end of the rail 10 and crosses the rail 10 transverse to its length, reaching from the rail 10 toward the workpiece edge 92. A downwardly projecting portion 32 of the fence 30 depends alongside the longitudinal side of the rail 10 that faces the workpiece edge 92, extends below the rail 10, and contacts the workpiece edge 92.

Two guide carriages, a first guide carriage 50 and a second guide carriage 70, rest on top of the rail 10, the first guide carriage 50 nearer the fence 30 and the second guide carriage 70 farther from the fence 30. Each guide carriage is shown schematically as a plain block. A clamp knob 58 stands above the first guide carriage 50 and a like clamp knob stands above the second guide carriage 70, each shown schematically as a plain round body above the rail 10.

## Numerals shown on this figure

- 10: rail
- 30: fence
- 32: downwardly projecting portion
- 50: first guide carriage
- 58: clamp knob of the first guide carriage
- 70: second guide carriage
- 90: workpiece
- 92: workpiece edge
