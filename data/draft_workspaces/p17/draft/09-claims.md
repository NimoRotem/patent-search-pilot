1. A drilling jig for locating and guiding a pair of holes in a workpiece having a front face and a straight edge, the drilling jig comprising:

an elongated straight rail having a flat lower face for resting on the front face, an upper face, a first end, a second end, a first longitudinal side, a second longitudinal side opposite the first longitudinal side, and a longitudinal slot extending through the rail from the upper face to the lower face, the upper face carrying distance marks alongside the longitudinal slot;

a fence releasably secured to the first end of the rail by two screws received in two corresponding holes in the fence, the fence being perpendicular to the rail and having a downwardly projecting portion that extends below the lower face for bearing against the straight edge, the fence being securable to the first end of the rail in a first orientation in which the downwardly projecting portion lies on the first longitudinal side of the rail and, upon removal of the two screws, inversion of the fence, and reinstallation of the two screws in the same two holes, in a second orientation in which the downwardly projecting portion lies on the second longitudinal side of the rail, the upper face and the distance marks carried on the upper face facing away from the front face in both the first orientation and the second orientation;

a first guide carriage and a second guide carriage seated separately on the upper face of the rail, each of the first guide carriage and the second guide carriage having a body and comprising:

a key depending into the longitudinal slot so as to prevent rotation of that guide carriage relative to the rail while permitting that guide carriage to slide along the longitudinal slot,

a cylindrical drill bushing having a bore that passes completely through that guide carriage and registers with the longitudinal slot, and

a clamp knob above the rail, the clamp knob having a threaded shank that passes through that guide carriage and through the longitudinal slot into a clamping shoe below the rail, whereby tightening the clamp knob draws the clamping shoe toward that guide carriage and clamps the rail between that guide carriage and the clamping shoe, and loosening the clamp knob permits that guide carriage to slide along the longitudinal slot;

the bore of the drill bushing of the first guide carriage and the bore of the drill bushing of the second guide carriage being parallel to one another and perpendicular to the flat lower face of the rail; and

a first pointer on the first guide carriage, the first pointer being aligned with an axis of the bore of the drill bushing of the first guide carriage and readable against the distance marks, and a second pointer on the second guide carriage, the second pointer being aligned with an axis of the bore of the drill bushing of the second guide carriage and readable against the distance marks.

18. A method of locating and guiding a pair of holes for a cabinet pull in a workpiece having a front face, a straight edge, and a centerline marked on the front face, the method comprising:

providing a drilling jig comprising an elongated straight rail having a flat lower face, an upper face carrying distance marks, a first end, a second end, a longitudinal slot extending through the rail from the upper face to the lower face, a transverse center index at a midpoint of the rail, and a sight notch formed in the second end of the rail and aligned with the transverse center index, a fence secured to the first end of the rail and having a downwardly projecting portion extending below the lower face, and a first guide carriage and a second guide carriage seated separately on the upper face, each guide carriage having a key received in the longitudinal slot that prevents rotation of that guide carriage relative to the rail, a drill bushing having a bore that passes completely through that guide carriage, a pointer aligned with an axis of that bore and readable against the distance marks, and a clamp knob having a threaded shank passing through that guide carriage and the longitudinal slot into a clamping shoe below the rail;

loosening the clamp knob of the first guide carriage and the clamp knob of the second guide carriage;

tightening the clamp knob of each of the first guide carriage and the second guide carriage so as to clamp the rail between that guide carriage and the clamping shoe of that guide carriage;

aligning the transverse center index and the sight notch with the centerline;

drilling through the drill bushing of the first guide carriage and through the drill bushing of the second guide carriage into the workpiece; and

lifting the drilling jig away from the workpiece, no part of the drilling jig remaining attached to the workpiece.
