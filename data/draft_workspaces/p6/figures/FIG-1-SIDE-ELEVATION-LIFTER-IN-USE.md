# FIG. 1 - Side elevation, lifter in use

**View type:** Side elevation view of the cordless handheld vacuum lifter 100 gripping a
workpiece 50. Solid outlines, no section. All line work lies well inside the drawing area,
clear of the filing margins.

**What is shown**

- The rigid body 14 is shown schematically as a compact closed enclosure in the middle of the
  drawing area.
- The handle 16 is shown schematically as a plain generic grip outline on the rigid body 14,
  carrying numeral 16.
- The suction cup 10 is shown schematically as a shallow dish carried by the rigid body 14.
- The flexible sealing lip 12 is on the suction cup 10 and is shown in contact with the slab
  surface.
- The workpiece 50 is a flat slab, broken off at each end with break lines to show
  indeterminate extent.

**Section designation**

A broken cutting-plane line is drawn for FIG. 2. It runs vertically, in the plane of the
drawing, centred left to right on the suction cup 10 and the handle 16, and is aligned with
the vertical centre line of the lifter. Its upper physical endpoint is beyond the handle 16 and
its lower physical endpoint is beyond the workpiece 50, both endpoints inside the drawing area.
An arrow at each endpoint points to the left, so that both arrows give the same viewing
direction. The repeated designation 2 is placed at both endpoints, so this is line 2-2. That
designation is drawing annotation rather than a reference numeral, and it carries no leader
line.

**Numeral anchors**

- 10 well inside the body of the suction cup dish.
- 12 at the lip where it meets the slab.
- 14 well inside the enclosure of the rigid body.
- 16 on the grip outline of the handle.
- 50 well inside the slab, to the right of the suction cup.
- 100 to the outline of the assembled lifter as a whole.
