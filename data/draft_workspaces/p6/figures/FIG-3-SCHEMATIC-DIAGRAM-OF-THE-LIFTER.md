# FIG. 3 - Schematic diagram of the lifter

**View type:** Schematic block diagram. Each element is shown schematically as a plain
rectangular block, except the suction cup 10 and the workpiece 50 as noted. All line work lies
well inside the drawing area, clear of the filing margins.

**What is shown**

- The suction cup 10 is shown schematically as a shallow open dish in the lower part of the
  drawing area, in contact with a flat slab which is the workpiece 50, the slab being broken
  off at each end.
- The port 22 is shown schematically as a plain opening through which the electric vacuum pump
  20 draws air from between the suction cup 10 and the workpiece 50.
- The electric vacuum pump 20, the pressure sensor 30, the warning indicator 32, and the
  rechargeable battery 40 are plain blocks arranged in the upper part of the drawing area,
  clear of the suction cup 10.
- A plain pneumatic line runs from the port 22 to the block of the electric vacuum pump 20,
  with an arrow on the line pointing from the port 22 toward the electric vacuum pump 20.
- A plain signal line runs from the block of the pressure sensor 30 to the block of the warning
  indicator 32, with an arrow on the line pointing toward the warning indicator 32.
- A plain power line runs from the block of the rechargeable battery 40 to the block of the
  electric vacuum pump 20.

**Numeral anchors**

- 10 well inside the wall of the dish.
- 20 well inside the pump block.
- 22 at the opening.
- 30 well inside the pressure sensor block.
- 32 well inside the warning indicator block.
- 40 well inside the battery block.
- 50 well inside the slab, clear of the dish.
