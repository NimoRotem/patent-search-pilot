# FIG. 4 - Flow diagram of the method of lifting a workpiece

**View type:** Flow diagram. Five plain rectangular boxes are arranged in a single vertical
column in the middle of the drawing area, with a plain arrow running from each box down to the
box below it. All line work lies well inside the drawing area, clear of the filing margins.

**What is shown**

The five boxes, from the top of the column to the bottom of the column, are the steps of the
method:

- The uppermost box is the placing step 202.
- The box immediately below the uppermost box is the evacuating step 204.
- The middle box is the carrying step 206.
- The box immediately above the lowest box is the monitoring step 208.
- The lowest box is the activating step 210.

The part names of the steps are semantic identifiers used to say which box each numeral
belongs to. They do not appear as drawing text, and no text is drawn inside the boxes.

**Numeral anchors**

- 202 well inside the uppermost box.
- 204 well inside the box immediately below the uppermost box.
- 206 well inside the middle box.
- 208 well inside the box immediately above the lowest box.
- 210 well inside the lowest box.
