# FIG. 2 - Vertical section through the lifter

**View type:** Vertical sectional view taken on line 2-2 of FIG. 1, viewed in the direction of
the arrows. Cut walls are cross-hatched. The electric vacuum pump 20 is shown in elevation as a
plain outlined block without hatching. All line work lies well inside the drawing area, clear
of the filing margins.

**What is shown**

- The rigid body 14 is shown in section as a hollow shell with hatched walls enclosing an
  interior cavity. Where the cutting plane meets the handle, the handle is shown cut,
  cross-hatched and unnumbered, in agreement with the generic grip outline of FIG. 1.
- The suction cup 10 is shown in section as a shallow dish with hatched walls carried by the
  rigid body 14, its open side facing the workpiece 50.
- The flexible sealing lip 12 is on the suction cup 10 and is shown in contact with the slab
  surface.
- The workpiece 50 is shown in section as a flat slab, broken off at each end.
- The port 22 is shown schematically as a plain opening through which the electric vacuum pump
  20 draws air.
- The electric vacuum pump 20 is a plain block inside the interior cavity of the rigid body 14.

**Numeral anchors**

- 10 well inside the hatched wall of the suction cup dish.
- 12 at the lip where it meets the slab.
- 14 well inside the hatched wall of the rigid body shell.
- 20 well inside the pump block.
- 22 at the opening.
- 50 well inside the slab, clear of the suction cup.
