Abstract: A materials handling device has a mounting frame, and vacuum modules mounted on the mounting frame. Each vacuum module has a cup, and an electrically powered vacuum pump. Each cup has a body, a rim to contact a workpiece, an internal region defined by the body and bounded by the rim, and a port that extends through the body and opens into the internal region. A fluid circuit interconnects the vacuum pump with the port. The vacuum pump is operable to draw gas from within the internal region through the fluid circuit to generate a partial vacuum within the internal region and reversibly attach the cup to the workpiece. An interface is configured to communicate with controllers of the vacuum modules. A user selects an operational mode of the vacuum modules; the state of the controllers is dependent on the selected operational mode.
