A cordless handheld vacuum lifter has a rigid body with a handle and a suction cup carried by the rigid body, the suction cup having a flexible sealing lip placed against a workpiece such as a glass panel or a stone panel. An electric vacuum pump within the rigid body draws air through a port to create a vacuum between the suction cup and the workpiece, by which a load is retained on the suction cup, a pressure sensor monitors the vacuum, and a rechargeable battery powers the pump. A warning indicator driven by the pressure sensor activates when the vacuum is lost, warning the user before the load can be dropped.
