FIG. 1 shows a cordless handheld vacuum lifter 100 in use. The cordless handheld vacuum lifter 100 comprises a rigid body 14 comprising a handle 16, and a suction cup 10 carried by the rigid body 14. The suction cup 10 comprises a flexible sealing lip 12. A workpiece 50, such as a glass panel or a stone panel, is gripped by the suction cup 10 and is carried by the user by way of the handle 16.

FIG. 2 is a vertical section through the cordless handheld vacuum lifter 100, showing the suction cup 10, the flexible sealing lip 12, the rigid body 14, an electric vacuum pump 20, a port 22, and the workpiece 50.

The suction cup 10 carries the flexible sealing lip 12. The flexible sealing lip 12 is placed against the workpiece 50.

The electric vacuum pump 20 is disposed within the rigid body 14. The electric vacuum pump 20 is configured to draw air through the port 22 so as to create a vacuum between the suction cup 10 and the workpiece 50. A load is retained on the suction cup 10 by the vacuum.

FIG. 3 shows the arrangement schematically. A pressure sensor 30 is configured to monitor the vacuum. A warning indicator 32 is driven by the pressure sensor 30. A rechargeable battery 40 is configured to power the electric vacuum pump 20. The electric vacuum pump 20 draws air through the port 22 from between the suction cup 10 and the workpiece 50.

The warning function operates as follows. The electric vacuum pump 20 creates the vacuum, the user lifts and carries the workpiece 50, and the load is retained on the suction cup 10 by the vacuum.

The warning indicator 32 activates when the vacuum is lost. The warning indicator 32 is configured to warn a user before the load can be dropped.

In embodiments, the workpiece 50 is a glass panel or a stone panel, the flexible sealing lip 12 being placed against the panel. Such a panel is the workpiece 50 for which the cordless handheld vacuum lifter 100 is principally intended.

A method of lifting a workpiece 50 with the cordless handheld vacuum lifter 100 is shown in FIG. 4. At a placing step 202, the flexible sealing lip 12 is placed against the workpiece 50. At an evacuating step 204, the electric vacuum pump 20, powered by the rechargeable battery 40, draws air through the port 22 and creates the vacuum between the suction cup 10 and the workpiece 50. At a carrying step 206, the workpiece 50 is lifted and a load is carried on the suction cup 10 by the handle 16, the load being retained on the suction cup 10 by the vacuum.

At a monitoring step 208, the pressure sensor 30 monitors the vacuum while the load is retained. At an activating step 210, the pressure sensor 30 drives the warning indicator 32 to activate when the vacuum is lost.
