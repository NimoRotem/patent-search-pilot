1. A cordless handheld vacuum lifter, comprising:

a rigid body comprising a handle;

a suction cup carried by the rigid body, the suction cup comprising a flexible sealing lip;

an electric vacuum pump disposed within the rigid body, the electric vacuum pump configured to draw air through a port so as to create a vacuum between the suction cup and a workpiece against which the flexible sealing lip is placed, a load being retained on the suction cup by the vacuum;

a pressure sensor configured to monitor the vacuum;

a warning indicator driven by the pressure sensor, the warning indicator configured to activate when the vacuum is lost; and

a rechargeable battery configured to power the electric vacuum pump.

2. The cordless handheld vacuum lifter of claim 1, wherein the warning indicator is configured to warn a user before the load can be dropped.

3. The cordless handheld vacuum lifter of claim 1, wherein the workpiece is a glass panel or a stone panel.

4. A method of lifting a workpiece with a cordless handheld vacuum lifter having a rigid body comprising a handle, a suction cup carried by the rigid body and comprising a flexible sealing lip, an electric vacuum pump disposed within the rigid body, a pressure sensor, a warning indicator, and a rechargeable battery, the method comprising:

placing the flexible sealing lip against the workpiece;

drawing air through a port with the electric vacuum pump powered by the rechargeable battery, so as to create a vacuum between the suction cup and the workpiece;

carrying a load on the suction cup by the handle, the load being retained on the suction cup by the vacuum;

monitoring the vacuum with the pressure sensor while the load is retained; and

activating the warning indicator, driven by the pressure sensor, when the vacuum is lost.

5. The method of claim 4, wherein activating the warning indicator warns a user before the load can be dropped.

6. The method of claim 4, wherein the workpiece is a glass panel or a stone panel.
