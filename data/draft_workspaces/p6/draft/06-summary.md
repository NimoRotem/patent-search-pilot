This summary introduces a selection of concepts that are described further in the detailed description. It is not intended to identify essential features of the claimed subject matter, nor to limit the scope of the claims.

A cordless handheld vacuum lifter is disclosed. The lifter has a rigid body comprising a handle, and a suction cup carried by the rigid body. The suction cup comprises a flexible sealing lip which is placed against a workpiece. An electric vacuum pump is disposed within the rigid body and draws air through a port so as to create a vacuum between the suction cup and the workpiece, by which vacuum the suction cup grips the workpiece and retains a load. A pressure sensor monitors the vacuum. A warning indicator is driven by the pressure sensor. A rechargeable battery powers the electric vacuum pump.

The warning indicator activates when the vacuum is lost.

In embodiments, the warning indicator warns a user before the load can be dropped. In embodiments, the workpiece is a glass panel or a stone panel.

A method of lifting a workpiece with such a lifter is also disclosed. The method includes placing the flexible sealing lip against the workpiece, drawing air through the port with the electric vacuum pump to create the vacuum, carrying the load on the suction cup by the handle, monitoring the vacuum with the pressure sensor while the load is retained, and activating the warning indicator when the vacuum is lost.
