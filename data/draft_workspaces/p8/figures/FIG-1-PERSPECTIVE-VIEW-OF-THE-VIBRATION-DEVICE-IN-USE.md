# FIG. 1 - Perspective view of the vibration device in use

**View:** Perspective view from above and to the front left. Outline line work only; the geometry carries no lettering, the numerals listed below being added in the annotation pass.

**Geometry to be drawn**

The covering element 36 is one large plain tile in perspective filling the lower drawing area, the machine standing on its left-hand part with open tile to the right.

The machine is one plain rectangular slab, the base 12, standing on a band that runs round its underside. Three closed blocks stand side by side on the top face of the slab: the left-hand block is the vibration motor 18, the middle block is the handle 44, and the right-hand block is the air-extraction mechanism 20. Slab, band, blocks and tile are shown schematically, and the order and spacing of the three blocks is a placement convention for this sheet only, no particular handle form, size or mounting arrangement being asserted.

The slab is drawn thick, its front face turned toward the viewer as a large plain surface. The view being taken from the front left, the left-hand end of the slab is also presented to the viewer, so that the slab and the band together present one continuous outer boundary on that side.

Each block is drawn broad and tall, its front face turned toward the viewer.

The band meets the underside of the slab and follows the same rectangular run, its front surface facing the viewer as one unbroken strip across the whole width and drawn deep, a broad clear surface standing between the underside of the slab above and the tile below, its upper and lower edges well apart; its depth is schematic. It stands on the tile, its lower edge meeting the tile.

The whole of the drawing stands well inside the drawing area, the machine drawn large within it.

- The vibration device 10 is the whole machine, slab, blocks and band together. Identified on the outer boundary line of the machine along the left-hand outer edge of the silhouette formed by the slab and the band, clear of the front face of the slab and of the blocks.
- The base 12 is the slab. Identified well inside its front face.
- The vibration motor 18 is the left-hand block. Identified well inside its front face.
- The air-extraction mechanism 20 is the right-hand block. Identified well inside its front face.
- The perimeter member 24 is the band beneath the slab. Identified well inside the front strip of the band, clear of its edges.
- The covering element 36 is the tile. Identified well inside the open tile to the right of the machine.
- The handle 44 is the middle block. Identified well inside its front face.
