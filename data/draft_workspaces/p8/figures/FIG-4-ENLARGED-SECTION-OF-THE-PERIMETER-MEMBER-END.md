# FIG. 4 - Enlarged section of the perimeter member end

**View:** Enlarged fragmentary section taken on line 4-4 of FIG. 3, through the lower end of the perimeter member where the clearance is present. Line work with hatching on cut material; the geometry carries no lettering, the numerals below being added in the annotation pass.

**Geometry to be drawn**

The sheet shows four hatched bodies: one upright column, the perimeter member 24, and three horizontal bands beneath it, all schematic, these plain rectangular forms being a convention for this sheet only.

The three bands are stacked in the lower part of the drawing area, together occupying about the lower half of its height, so each is drawn deep. Each runs across the drawing area, ending just inside its left and right limits, filled with regularly spaced parallel hatching continuous from side to side, including beneath the column; the depths are schematic. The uppermost band is the covering element 36, the middle band is the bonding material 40, the lowest is the substrate 42. Each band lies on the one below it, with a plain solid line along each join, so every band reads as a separate hatched body; no extent of contact is asserted.

The column stands above the uppermost band, an open stretch of that band on each side of it, rising from one horizontal line closing it below to just inside the upper limit of the drawing area, filled with regularly spaced parallel hatching. The flat form of its lower end is a depiction convention for this sheet only, no particular shape of the bearing face 26 being asserted.

Between the bottom line of the column and the top line of the uppermost band lies open unhatched space, shown enlarged for clarity, both lines drawn plainly, deep enough for a point well inside it to stand clear of both.
