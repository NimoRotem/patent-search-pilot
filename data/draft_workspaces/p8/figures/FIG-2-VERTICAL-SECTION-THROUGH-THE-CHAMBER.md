# FIG. 2 - Vertical section through the chamber

**View:** Vertical section taken on line 2-2 of FIG. 3, through the vibration device of FIG. 1 and the covering element 36 beneath it, where the perimeter member bears on it. Line work with hatching on cut material; the geometry carries no lettering, the numerals listed below being added in the annotation pass. The vibration motor and the handle are omitted; the clearance appears only in FIG. 4.

**Geometry to be drawn**

The sheet shows four schematic bodies: one hatched horizontal slab, the base 12; one closed loop cut twice, appearing as two hatched legs hanging from the underside of the slab, one at each end, so the loop runs at the perimeter of the underside, each leg drawn deep enough for its hatching and an interior point to read clearly, its depth schematic; one hatched band across the bottom, the covering element 36, on which the legs stand; and one housing standing on the slab, the air-extraction mechanism 20.

The slab, the legs and the band are the cut bodies, each filled with regularly spaced parallel hatching. Where two of them meet, a plain solid line runs along the join, so each reads as a separate hatched body. The housing lies outside the cut, in plain unhatched outline. A plain unhatched gap runs through the slab beneath the housing, from the inside of the housing to the chamber 22, so that the air-extraction mechanism 20 reads as being in fluid communication with the chamber 22; the gap is schematic, its width, position and form being a convention for this sheet only, no particular port, duct or valve construction being asserted.

The slab is drawn thick, its upper face visible each side of the housing, and each leg rests on the band.

The whole drawing stands well inside the drawing area.

- The base 12 is the horizontal hatched slab. Identified well inside its hatching.
- The first side 14 is the upper face of the base 12, seen edge on as the upper edge line of the slab. Identified at that line clear of the housing, a point touching it from either side counting as on it.
- The air-extraction mechanism 20 is the unhatched housing. Identified well inside it.
- The chamber 22 is the open space between the legs, below the base 12 and above the band. Identified well inside it.
- The perimeter member 24 is the closed loop hanging from the base 12, appearing as the two legs. Identified well inside the hatching of the right-hand leg.
- The covering element 36 is the bottom band. Identified well inside its hatching.
