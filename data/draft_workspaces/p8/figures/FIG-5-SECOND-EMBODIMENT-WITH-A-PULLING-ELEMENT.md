# FIG. 5 - Second embodiment with a pulling element

**View:** Perspective view from above and to the front left, as FIG. 1. Outline line work only; the geometry carries no lettering, the numerals below being added in the annotation pass. The vibration motor and the air-extraction mechanism are omitted.

**Geometry to be drawn**

The covering element 36 is one large plain tile in perspective, as in FIG. 1, filling the lower drawing area. The machine stands on its right-hand part, with open tile to the left.

The machine is one plain rectangular body standing on a band that runs round its underside, the band alone touching the tile, machine, band and tile being shown schematically.

The band meets the underside of the body on the same rectangular run, its front surface facing the viewer as one unbroken strip across the whole width and drawn deep, its depth schematic. Its lower edge meets the tile.

The rectangular body is drawn broad and tall, its front face turned toward the viewer as a large plain surface. The view being taken from the front left, the body's left-hand end is presented to the viewer, the body and band sharing one continuous outer edge on that side, the right-hand end reading as one boundary edge between the top face and the band.

The flexible pulling element 46 is one long cord, shown schematically, no particular thickness or construction being asserted. It begins at the left-hand side of the machine, that meeting shown schematically with no attachment formation, and runs away to the left over the open tile, ending well inside the left-hand limit of the drawing area. Its middle third is drawn as one straight stretch running across the upper part of the open tile, held clear of every other line, with a broad expanse of open tile below it along the whole of its length, the two shorter end stretches curving gently to meet the machine on the right and to reach the free end on the left. The cord crosses and touches no other line.

The whole of the drawing stands well inside the drawing area.

- The vibration device 10 is the whole machine, the rectangular body with its band. Identified on the outer boundary line at the right-hand end of the body, clear of the cord.
- The flexible pulling element 46 is that cord. Identified anywhere along the straight middle stretch, clear of the two curving end stretches, a point touching that stretch from either side counting as on it; the numeral stands in open tile above the cord, clear of the machine and of the numeral 36.
- The covering element 36 is that large plain tile. Identified well inside the broad open tile below the straight middle stretch and to the left of the machine, well clear of every edge of the tile and of the cord.
