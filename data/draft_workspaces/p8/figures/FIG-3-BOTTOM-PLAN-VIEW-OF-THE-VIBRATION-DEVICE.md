# FIG. 3 - Bottom plan view of the vibration device

**View:** Plan view looking straight up at the underside of the machine, nothing beneath the machine being drawn. Outline line work only; the geometry carries no lettering, the numerals listed below and the section designations being added in the annotation pass.

**Geometry to be drawn**

The sheet shows the underside of the machine as one rectangular ring, like a picture frame. Its line work is: the outer edge of the ring, the inner edge held within it, and the two section lines. The surface between those edges runs unbroken from one to the other along every side, so the view reads as one continuous closed frame; the drawn width of that frame is a depiction convention for this sheet only, no particular width of the perimeter member 24 being asserted. The rectangular plan form is a convention for this sheet only, both parts shown schematically.

The perimeter member 24 is the ring surface. It runs all the way round, carrying round each corner without interruption, so it reads as one closed loop, broad enough for a point well inside it to clear both edges.

The second side 16 is the face at which this view looks straight, appearing as the area within the inner edge, broad enough for a point well inside it to clear the edge.

Beyond the outer edge lies background. The ring stands well inside the drawing area.

Two broken section lines cross the view. Each passes through the face within the inner edge, cutting the ring surface on both sides of that face, and each ends in the background past the outer edge but short of the limits of the drawing area, so both ends stay within the drawing area. One line is horizontal and the other is vertical. Where each line is drawn on this sheet is a placement convention for this sheet only.

The horizontal line carries a short arrow at each end, both pointing toward the lower part of the drawing area, and the numeral 2 at each end, so it reads as section line 2-2 and leads to the sectional view of FIG. 2.

The vertical line carries a short arrow at each end, both pointing toward the left-hand part of the drawing area, and the numeral 4 at each end, so it reads as section line 4-4 and leads to the sectional view of FIG. 4.

- The perimeter member 24 is the ring surface between the two outlines. Identified well inside it along the left-hand side of the ring, clear of both section lines.
- The second side 16 is the face within the inner edge. Identified well inside it, clear of the inner edge and of both section lines.

**Numerals appearing on this figure:** 16, 24
