A vibration device for bedding covering elements into thinset, mortar or adhesive has a rigid base carrying a vibration motor and an air-extraction mechanism on its first side and a chamber on its second side. A rigid perimeter member of low-friction material such as plastic or PTFE surrounds the chamber, its end face forming a bearing face. It is not a seal: a clearance remains against the exposed face, so ambient air enters the chamber continuously. Air is withdrawn fast enough to keep the chamber below atmospheric pressure while air keeps entering, and the pressure differential presses the device onto the covering element without downward force from the operator. Every member between the motor and the covering element is rigid, so vibration transfers with substantially less damping than through a flexible suction cup, and the bearing face lets the device slide to successive areas without releasing the hold.
