1. A vibration device for bedding a covering element into a bonding material, the vibration device comprising:

a base that is rigid and has a first side and a second side opposite the first side;

a vibration motor coupled to the first side;

a perimeter member extending from the second side at a perimeter thereof and surrounding a chamber located on the second side, the perimeter member being rigid and of a low-friction material and terminating, remote from the base, in a bearing face that is an end face of the perimeter member, the chamber being bounded by the second side, the perimeter member and, when the bearing face is placed against an exposed face of the covering element, by the exposed face, and a clearance remaining, along at least part of the perimeter member, between the bearing face and the exposed face, through which clearance ambient air enters the chamber; and

an air-extraction mechanism coupled to the first side and in fluid communication with the chamber, the air-extraction mechanism being operable to withdraw air from the chamber at a volumetric extraction rate sufficient to maintain the chamber below atmospheric pressure while the ambient air continues to enter the chamber through the clearance, a resulting pressure differential holding the vibration device against the covering element;

wherein a vibration transmission path from the vibration motor to the bearing face passes through the base and the perimeter member, each member of the vibration transmission path is rigid, and no elastomeric member, foam member or resilient mount is interposed in the vibration transmission path; and

wherein the bearing face is slidable over the exposed face while the pressure differential is maintained.

2. The vibration device of claim 1, wherein the perimeter member is a closed loop that runs around the chamber, the closed loop restricting a flow of the ambient air into the chamber through the clearance without closing the clearance and without forming a complete airtight seal.

3. The vibration device of claim 1, wherein the low-friction material of the perimeter member is plastic, metal, polytetrafluoroethylene, or a combination of rigid materials.

4. The vibration device of claim 1, wherein the perimeter member is free of rubber, elastomer and foam, and makes rigid contact with the exposed face.

5. The vibration device of claim 1, wherein the pressure differential produces a holding force sufficient to hold the vibration device against the exposed face without a downward force being applied by an operator.

6. The vibration device of claim 1, wherein the air-extraction mechanism is operable to withdraw air from the chamber continuously while the vibration device is in use.

7. The vibration device of claim 1, wherein the air-extraction mechanism is a vacuum pump.

8. The vibration device of claim 1, further comprising a handle arranged to guide the vibration device over the exposed face while the pressure differential holds the vibration device against the covering element.

9. The vibration device of claim 1, further comprising a flexible pulling element connected to the vibration device, the vibration device being translatable over the exposed face by tension applied to the flexible pulling element while the pressure differential is maintained.
