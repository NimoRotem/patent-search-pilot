# FIG. 4

Enlarged view of the lower side region of FIG. 1 enclosed by the broken-line curve there designated FIG. 4, seen horizontally in section. Plain black line work on white, section hatching on cut material. All line work lies well inside the drawing area, clear of the margins.

A thick hatched upright insulating wall body occupies the left portion of the drawing area and runs the full height of the fragment. Its inner surface faces right. The inward projections of the wall bodies lie outside this fragment.

In the upper right part of the drawing area, a hatched portion of the sectioned spacer frame is drawn in section. Its left face is spaced from the inner surface of the upright wall body, leaving an open vertical space between them. An open vertical channel passes down through this sectioned spacer frame, with hatched material on each side apart from the openings described below. This channel is one of the guide ducts 50. At the lower end of that channel an opening passes through the downwardly facing bottom surface of the sectioned spacer frame, as in FIG. 5.

Where that channel meets the open vertical space, an opening passes through the left face of the sectioned spacer frame, so that the channel opens sideways into that space between the frame and the upright wall body. This opening is one of the peripheral outlet openings 54.

Below the sectioned spacer frame, and spaced to the right of the inner surface of the upright wall body, a thin hatched upright wall is drawn in section and runs downward to the thin horizontal bottom wall described below. This thin wall is one of the tray side walls 26. The open vertical space continues down between the upright wall body and the thin wall, so that the space fed through the peripheral outlet opening 54 runs downward past the thin wall. This continuing space is one of the side supply passages 28.

A thin hatched horizontal bottom wall joins the thin upright wall and extends rightward, and the open space beneath that bottom wall is the lower return passage 24.

Where the thin upright wall meets that bottom wall, a series of small openings passes through the product tray, so that the side supply passage 28 opens through them into the space beneath the bottom wall. These openings are the perforations 30.

## Numerals shown on this figure

- 24: inside the open space beneath the thin horizontal bottom wall.
- 26: well inside the hatching of the thin upright wall, above the small openings.
- 28: inside the open vertical space between the upright wall body and the thin wall.
- 30: on one of the small openings through the product tray.
- 50: inside the open vertical channel in the sectioned spacer frame.
- 54: on the opening through the left face of the sectioned spacer frame.
