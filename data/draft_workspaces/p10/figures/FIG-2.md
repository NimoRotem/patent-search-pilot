# FIG. 2

Perspective view of the thermal module alone, seen from above and in front so the open top, the near long side wall and the right short end wall are visible. Plain black line work on white, no hatching, all inside the drawing area.

The main body is a rectangular open frame, shown schematically, with near and far long side walls, left and right short end walls and a bottom panel joining them, its top open. This frame is the spacer frame 42; with the two slabs it holds it is the thermal module 40.

Two flat rectangular slabs stand upright inside the frame, parallel to the long side walls, their extent along the frame shown schematically. They are spaced apart across the frame depth, leaving an open central gap between their confronting broad faces, and each is spaced from the adjacent long side wall, leaving an open vertical channel between slab and wall. The slab nearer the front is the first phase-change cassette 44, the slab nearer the back the second phase-change cassette 46, each a plain closed box, shown schematically.

In the bottom panel, within the open central gap between the slabs, a rectangular through opening is drawn, so the viewer looks down through the panel there. This opening is the central inlet opening 48.

A further through opening passes down through the bottom panel at the foot of the channel beside each slab, so the viewer looks down through the panel there and each channel is open at its lower end.

Along the lower part of the near long side wall, at the foot of the channel beside the first phase-change cassette 44, a rectangular opening passes through the wall. This is one of the peripheral outlet openings 54. A matching opening in the far long side wall is concealed behind the second phase-change cassette 46.

The top edge of the near long side wall carries an upwardly open groove, shown schematically. This groove is the mating channel 56.

Each long side wall carries a downwardly facing surface at its outboard side, shown schematically. Small blocks project down from that surface, two visible at the front, shown schematically. These blocks are the resilient feet 58.

## Numerals shown on this figure

- 40: outside the frame outline, with a lead line and arrow to the module as a whole.
- 42: on the right short end wall, well inside its outline.
- 44: well inside the visible upper face of the front slab.
- 46: well inside the visible upper face of the rear slab.
- 48: well inside the opening in the bottom panel between the slabs.
- 54: on the opening in the near long side wall.
- 56: well inside the groove in the top edge of the near long side wall.
- 58: well inside one of the small blocks projecting down from the downwardly facing outboard surface at the front.
