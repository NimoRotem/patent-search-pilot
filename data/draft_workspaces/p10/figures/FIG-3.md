# FIG. 3

Enlarged view of the upper side region of FIG. 1 enclosed by the broken-line curve there designated FIG. 3, seen horizontally in section with the lid closed. Plain black line work on white, diagonal section hatching on cut material. All line work lies well inside the drawing area, clear of the margins.

A thick hatched upright wall body occupies the left portion of the drawing area, running from an upper end down to the lower edge of the fragment, its outer surface facing left and its inner surface facing right. This is one of the side walls 14 of the sectioned outer shell. Partway down the inner surface, a hatched block projects rightward from it with an upwardly facing top surface, shown schematically. This projection is one of the ledges 18.

Across the upper part of the drawing area lies a thick hatched horizontal closure slab, shown in section, spanning rightward past the middle of the drawing area, its underside facing the top end of the upright wall body.

A small hatched body of compressible material lies between the underside of the closure slab and the top end surface of the upright wall body, contacting both and flattened between them. This body is the compressible lid gasket 64.

Inboard of the compressible lid gasket 64, a narrow rib projects downward from the underside of the closure slab, extending below the level of the top end of the upright wall body. This projection is the depending indexing rib 62.

To the right of the upright wall body, spaced from its inner surface, a hatched body is drawn in section extending from beneath the closure slab downward past the lower edge of the fragment, so that it is shown cut off there. This body is the sectioned spacer frame 42. Its upper edge carries an upwardly open groove that receives the lower end of the depending indexing rib 62. This groove is the mating channel 56.

At the level of the ledge 18, a small block lies between a downwardly facing outboard surface of the sectioned spacer frame 42 and the upwardly facing top surface of the ledge 18, contacting both and flattened between them. This block is one of the resilient feet 58.

## Numerals shown on this figure

- 14: well inside the hatching of the upright wall body, below the closure slab.
- 18: well inside the block projecting from the inner surface of the upright wall body.
- 42: well inside the sectioned frame body, clear of the groove.
- 56: inside the groove in the upper edge of the frame body.
- 58: well inside the block resting on the top surface of the ledge.
- 62: well inside the narrow downward projection of the closure slab.
- 64: well inside the compressible body between the closure slab and the wall body.
