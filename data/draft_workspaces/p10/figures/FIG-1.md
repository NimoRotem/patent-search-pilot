# FIG. 1

Schematic vertical section of an assembled carrier, on a plane cutting both upright blocks in the module body and the groove in the top of its left side portion. Plain black line work, hatching on cut material, inside the drawing area.

Two thick hatched upright wall bodies joined by a thick hatched bottom form a container open at top: the sectioned outer shell 10, its bottom the floor 12. Each wall body carries an inward-projecting block below its top. A thick hatched slab across both top ends is the insulated lid 60, closed, a narrow rib projecting down inboard of the left end and a compressible body between slab and each end.

Within it, a sectioned open-top box of thin hatched walls and bottom is the product tray 20, held above the floor 12 by short hatched blocks, two appearing here, shown schematically, the tray feet 22. Its walls stand clear of the wall bodies, leaving an open vertical gap each side, with small openings through the tray where each gap meets the space beneath. One shallow trough runs around the tray, cut at both gaps so it appears at each, one elongated absorbent body appearing in both, a narrow strip at one end of the right-hand appearance.

Above the tray, below the lid 60, a sectioned hatched body is the thermal module 40: two cut upright side portions joined by a hatched bottom, the space between the side portions open at the top beneath the lid 60, the bottom open downward at its centre, the body holding two upright hatched blocks, shown schematically, spaced apart. An open vertical channel runs down outboard of each block, opening through the body's outboard side above the gap, and at its downward lower end over the trough. A small block is flattened between a down-facing outboard surface of the body and each projecting block. A groove opening upward in the cut top surface of the left side portion takes the rib's lower end.

Three broken-line curves enclose: upper left, the rib, groove, wall top, compressible body, projecting block and small block; lower left, the outboard channel opening, the channel lower end and downward opening, gap, tray openings, tray bottom wall and space under it; lower right, the outboard channel opening, the channel lower end and downward opening, the tray wall with its openings, gap, bottom body, trough, absorbent body, strip.
