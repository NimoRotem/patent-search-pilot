# FIG. 7

Enlarged schematic sectional view of the mechanical exposure indicator, taken on line 7-7 of FIG. 6, with the indicator stem in the exposed position. Plain black line work on white, section hatching on cut material. All line work lies well inside the drawing area, clear of the margins.

A thick hatched upright wall body stands across the middle of the drawing area, its outer surface facing left and its inner surface facing right. This wall body is one of the side walls 14 of the sectioned outer shell 10.

Carried by the wall body, and drawn as a horizontally elongated plain rectangular outline within the wall body and open at its outer end, is the body of the mechanical exposure indicator 80, shown schematically.

An elongated horizontal shaft lies within that outline and projects to the left past the outer surface of the wall body. This shaft is the indicator stem 82.

A spring, shown schematically as a coil of aligned loops within the outline and drawn extended, acts on the shaft in the direction of its outward projection. This spring is the spring 86.

A small block within the outline beside the shaft, shown schematically, is spaced from the shaft, the latch being shown released. This block is the temperature-responsive latch 84.

## Numerals shown on this figure

- 10: outside the wall body, clear of line work, with a lead line and arrow to the outer shell as a whole.
- 14: well inside the hatching of the upright wall body, below the indicator outline.
- 80: on the outline of the indicator body carried by the wall body, clear of the shaft.
- 82: on the part of the shaft projecting left beyond the outer surface of the wall body.
- 84: well inside the small block beside the shaft.
- 86: on one loop of the spring.
