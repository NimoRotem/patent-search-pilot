A passive cold-chain carrier has an insulated outer shell, a product tray on feet above the shell floor leaving a lower return passage, and tray side walls spaced inward from the shell side walls forming two side supply passages. A thermal module has a rigid spacer frame holding two opposed phase-change cassettes and defining a central inlet opening, two downward guide ducts, peripheral outlet openings and a mating channel. A depending indexing rib on an insulated lid enters the mating channel as the lid closes, shifting the module into a registered position that aligns the outlet openings with the side supply passages. Without a powered air mover, air rises from the product region into the central inlet opening, divides along the cassette faces, descends the guide ducts and side supply passages, and returns through tray perforations to the lower return passage.
