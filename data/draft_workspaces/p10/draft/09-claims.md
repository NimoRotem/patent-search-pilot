1. A passive cold-chain carrier comprising:

an insulated outer shell having a floor, side walls and an upper edge;

a product tray removably received in the outer shell, the product tray having a bottom wall, tray side walls, perforations, and tray feet standing on the floor so that the product tray is supported above the floor and a lower return passage remains between the bottom wall and the floor, the tray side walls being spaced inward from the side walls of the outer shell so that two side supply passages are formed;

a thermal module removably received in the outer shell above the product tray, the thermal module comprising a rigid spacer frame and two opposed phase-change cassettes held by the spacer frame, the spacer frame defining a central inlet opening, two downward guide ducts located beside the phase-change cassettes, peripheral outlet openings positioned to align with the side supply passages, and a mating channel; and

an insulated lid closable on the upper edge of the outer shell and carrying a depending indexing rib positioned to enter the mating channel as the insulated lid closes and to shift the thermal module into a registered position in which the peripheral outlet openings overlap the side supply passages;

the carrier being free of a fan, a pump and any powered air mover, and the outer shell, the product tray, the thermal module and the insulated lid cooperating in the registered position to define a circulation path along which air rising from a product region within the product tray enters the central inlet opening, divides along faces of the two phase-change cassettes, descends through the guide ducts, enters the side supply passages, passes through the perforations, and returns through the lower return passage.

2. The passive cold-chain carrier of claim 1, wherein the spacer frame carries resilient feet, the outer shell has ledges beneath the resilient feet, and closure of the insulated lid presses the resilient feet against the ledges so that the thermal module is held down against lifting away from the product tray.

3. The passive cold-chain carrier of claim 1, further comprising a compressible lid gasket carried by the insulated lid and arranged to seal against the upper edge of the outer shell after the depending indexing rib has shifted the thermal module into the registered position.

4. The passive cold-chain carrier of claim 1, wherein the two opposed phase-change cassettes are held by the spacer frame with an inner face of a first phase-change cassette confronting an inner face of a second phase-change cassette across a central space, and the central inlet opening opens downward into the central space.

5. The passive cold-chain carrier of claim 1, wherein the phase-change cassettes are removable from the spacer frame.

6. The passive cold-chain carrier of claim 1, further comprising a shallow condensate channel extending around the product tray, and a removable absorbent wick lying in the condensate channel.

7. The passive cold-chain carrier of claim 6, wherein the spacer frame further defines discharge openings at lower ends of the guide ducts, the discharge openings discharging condensate into the condensate channel.

8. The passive cold-chain carrier of claim 6, wherein the absorbent wick carries a pull tab that remains accessible when the product tray is removed from the outer shell, so that the absorbent wick is replaceable without opening a phase-change cassette.

9. The passive cold-chain carrier of claim 1, further comprising a mechanical exposure indicator carried by the outer shell and visible from outside the carrier, the mechanical exposure indicator comprising an indicator stem thermally coupled to one of the phase-change cassettes, a temperature-responsive latch retaining the indicator stem in a stowed position, and a spring biasing the indicator stem toward an exposed position, the temperature-responsive latch releasing the indicator stem when said one of the phase-change cassettes softens.

10. The passive cold-chain carrier of claim 9, wherein the exposed position is a latched position in which the mechanical exposure indicator retains the indicator stem after said one of the phase-change cassettes has cooled again, the mechanical exposure indicator being resettable manually only after the carrier is opened.

11. The passive cold-chain carrier of claim 9, wherein the mechanical exposure indicator does not control airflow, the circulation path being the same whether or not the indicator stem stands in the exposed position.

12. The passive cold-chain carrier of claim 1, wherein the depending indexing rib meets the mating channel while the insulated lid is spaced above the upper edge of the outer shell, and remaining closing travel of the insulated lid translates the spacer frame horizontally into the registered position.

a rigid spacer frame sized to be removably received in the outer shell above the product tray; and

two opposed phase-change cassettes held by the spacer frame;

the spacer frame defining a central inlet opening through which air rising from a product region within the product tray enters the spacer frame, two downward guide ducts located beside the phase-change cassettes and receiving air that has divided along faces of the two phase-change cassettes, peripheral outlet openings positioned to align with the side supply passages, and a mating channel positioned to be entered by the depending indexing rib as the insulated lid closes so that the thermal module is shifted into a registered position in which the peripheral outlet openings overlap the side supply passages.

14. The thermal module of claim 13, further comprising resilient feet carried by the spacer frame, the resilient feet being positioned to be pressed against ledges of the outer shell by closure of the insulated lid so that the thermal module is held down against lifting away from the product tray.

15. The thermal module of claim 13, wherein the mating channel opens upward at a top region of the spacer frame.

16. The thermal module of claim 13, wherein the spacer frame further defines discharge openings at lower ends of the guide ducts and positioned to discharge condensate into a condensate channel extending around the product tray within the outer shell.

17. A method of transporting temperature-sensitive goods, the method comprising:

fitting two conditioned phase-change cassettes to a rigid spacer frame of a thermal module, the spacer frame defining a central inlet opening, two downward guide ducts located beside the phase-change cassettes, peripheral outlet openings, and a mating channel;

placing the goods in a product tray and placing the product tray in an insulated outer shell so that tray feet of the product tray stand on a floor of the outer shell, leaving a lower return passage between the product tray and the floor and two side supply passages between tray side walls of the product tray and side walls of the outer shell;

placing the thermal module in the outer shell above the product tray;

closing an insulated lid on an upper edge of the outer shell so that a depending indexing rib carried by the insulated lid enters the mating channel and shifts the thermal module into a registered position in which the peripheral outlet openings overlap the side supply passages; and

thereafter transporting the goods while air circulates, without operation of any powered air mover, along a circulation path in which air rising from a product region within the product tray enters the central inlet opening, divides along faces of the two phase-change cassettes, descends through the guide ducts, enters the side supply passages, passes through perforations in the product tray, and returns through the lower return passage.

18. The method of claim 17, wherein closing the insulated lid further presses resilient feet of the spacer frame against ledges of the outer shell, retaining the thermal module against lifting away from the product tray during the transporting, and compresses a compressible lid gasket against the upper edge of the outer shell after the thermal module has been shifted into the registered position.

19. The method of claim 17, further comprising, after the transporting, opening the insulated lid, removing the product tray from the outer shell, and drawing a removable absorbent wick, by a pull tab of the absorbent wick, out of a condensate channel extending around the product tray within the outer shell, without opening a phase-change cassette.

20. The method of claim 17, further comprising reading, from outside the outer shell, a mechanical exposure indicator having an indicator stem thermally coupled to one of the phase-change cassettes, the indicator stem having been moved by a spring to a latched exposed position on release of a temperature-responsive latch by softening of said one of the phase-change cassettes and remaining in the latched exposed position after said one of the phase-change cassettes has cooled again, and resetting the mechanical exposure indicator manually after the insulated lid has been opened.
