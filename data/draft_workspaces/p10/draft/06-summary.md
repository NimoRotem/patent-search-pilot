A passive cold-chain carrier is disclosed in which the closing of the lid, rather than the care of the packer, establishes the circulation path.

In one aspect, the carrier includes an insulated outer shell having a floor and side walls, a product tray removably received in the outer shell, a thermal module removably received in the outer shell above the product tray, and an insulated lid. The product tray is supported above the floor by tray feet so that a lower return passage remains between the product tray and the floor, and the tray side walls are spaced inward from the side walls of the outer shell to form two side supply passages. The thermal module includes a rigid spacer frame and two opposed phase-change cassettes held by the spacer frame, the spacer frame defining a central inlet opening, two downward guide ducts beside the cassettes, and peripheral outlet openings. The insulated lid carries a depending indexing rib positioned to enter a mating channel of the spacer frame as the lid closes and to shift the thermal module into a registered position in which the peripheral outlet openings overlap the side supply passages.

In the registered position the shell, the product tray, the thermal module and the lid cooperate to define a circulation path along which air rising from a product region within the product tray enters the central inlet opening, divides along faces of the two phase-change cassettes, descends through the guide ducts, enters the side supply passages, passes through perforations in the product tray, and returns through the lower return passage. The carrier is free of a fan, a pump and any powered air mover.

The technical effect of lid-driven registration is that the alignment on which the circulation path depends is created by an act the packer cannot omit. The thermal module is placed in the outer shell above the product tray, and the depending indexing rib entering the mating channel shifts the module into the position in which the peripheral outlet openings overlap the side supply passages. The seal made by a compressible lid gasket against the upper edge of the outer shell occurs after registration, so the carrier is not closed against a module that is still out of position. The same closing movement presses resilient feet of the spacer frame against ledges of the outer shell, so that the registered module is held down against the product tray and cannot lift away from it during transport.

In a further aspect, a shallow condensate channel extends around the product tray and receives a removable absorbent wick. Discharge openings at lower ends of the guide ducts deliver condensate into the condensate channel. A pull tab on the absorbent wick remains accessible when the product tray is removed, so the wick is replaced without opening a phase-change cassette and without taking the cassettes out of service.

In a further aspect, the outer shell carries a mechanical exposure indicator visible from outside the carrier. An indicator stem is thermally coupled to one of the phase-change cassettes and is retained by a temperature-responsive latch. If that cassette softens enough to release the latch, a spring moves the indicator stem to a latched exposed position, and the indication remains exposed after the cassette cools again, being reset manually only after the carrier is opened. The indicator therefore reports the state of the refrigerant on which the payload depends, survives recovery of the cassette, and requires no battery. The exposure indicator does not control airflow, so the circulation path is unaffected by whether the indicator has tripped.

In a further aspect, a method includes fitting conditioned phase-change cassettes to the spacer frame, placing product in the product tray, placing the product tray in the outer shell, placing the thermal module above the product tray, and closing the insulated lid so that the depending indexing rib enters the mating channel and shifts the thermal module into the registered position, whereafter air circulates along the circulation path without operation of any powered air mover.

The condensate channel and absorbent wick with its pull tab, and the mechanical exposure indicator, are optional features that may be provided together or separately, and the summary is not a statement of the scope of the claims.
