FIG. 1 is a front elevation of a split pipe clamp closed around a pipe, showing the hinge, the releasable latch, the jaw carriages and the mounting boss.

FIG. 2 is a front interior view of the closed split pipe clamp, with near-side frame structure omitted to reveal the jaw carriages in their radial guides, the annular guide for the cam ring, and the mounting boss.

FIG. 3 is a front view of the segmented cam ring alone, removed from the frame and laid out as it stands when the frame halves are closed, showing the oblique slots and the ring drive face, the drive faces at the segment junctions being identified in FIG. 6.

FIG. 4 is an enlarged cutaway view of the latch, showing the latch handle, the force-limiting spring stack, the indicator pin, and the wedge bearing on the ring drive face.

FIG. 5 is an enlarged detail view of a single jaw carriage, showing the jaw pad, the carriage spring, and the follower that engages an oblique slot in the cam ring.

FIG. 6 is a further front view of the segmented cam ring of FIG. 3, showing the complementary hinge-end and latch-end drive faces that engage at the junctions between the segments.
