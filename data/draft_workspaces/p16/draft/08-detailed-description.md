In the following description, like reference numerals designate like parts throughout the several views. The described arrangement is one form of the invention and is not a limitation on the scope of the claims. Terms of orientation such as inward, outward, radial and circumferential are used with reference to a central axis of the closed frame.

Overall arrangement

Referring to FIG. 1, a split pipe clamp 10 comprises a first frame half 12 and a second frame half 14. The first frame half 12 and the second frame half 14 are each substantially semicircular, and together they define a frame that surrounds a pipe 20 when closed. The first frame half 12 and the second frame half 14 are joined at a hinge end of the frame by a hinge 16 and are releasably joined at a latch end of the frame, opposite the hinge end, by a releasable latch 18. Swinging the first frame half 12 and the second frame half 14 apart about the hinge 16 opens the frame laterally, so that the split pipe clamp 10 can be placed around the pipe 20. The split pipe clamp 10 and the pipe 20 together form an installed clamp assembly when the split pipe clamp 10 is closed and clamped on the pipe 20.

Three jaw carriages 22 are distributed circumferentially about the closed frame.

The frame carries a mounting boss 66. The mounting boss 66 supports a sensor, a tool or a conduit on the split pipe clamp 10. The mounting boss 66 does not participate in synchronizing the jaw carriages 22.

Jaw carriages and jaw pads

Referring to FIGS. 2 and 5, each jaw carriage 22 is slidable in a respective radial guide 24 of the frame. The radial guide 24 constrains the jaw carriage 22 to travel along a radial path toward and away from the central axis of the closed frame.

Each jaw carriage 22 carries a jaw pad 26 that is mounted on the jaw carriage 22 for pivoting movement and that presents a concave bearing surface toward the central axis of the closed frame. Because the jaw pad 26 pivots relative to the jaw carriage 22, the jaw pad 26 bears on the pipe 20 without requiring a tool specific pipe diameter.

A carriage spring 42 biases each jaw carriage 22 outward, away from the central axis of the closed frame. When the releasable latch 18 is released, the carriage springs 42 retract the jaw carriages 22 outward and lift the jaw pads 26 off the pipe 20 before the frame halves are opened.

The segmented cam ring

A cam ring 30 is retained in an annular guide 36 of the frame. The annular guide 36, shown in FIG. 2, retains the cam ring 30 while allowing the cam ring 30 to rotate through a limited stroke about the central axis of the closed frame.

The mounting boss 66 stands on the frame outside the annular guide 36.

The cam ring 30 is segmented. Referring to FIG. 3, the cam ring 30 comprises a first cam-ring segment 32 and a second cam-ring segment 34. The first cam-ring segment 32 is carried by the first frame half 12 and the second cam-ring segment 34 is carried by the second frame half 14, each segment being retained by the frame half that carries it. Each segment accordingly stays with its own frame half when the frame is opened.

The first cam-ring segment 32 has a first hinge-end drive face 44 and a first latch-end drive face 48. The second cam-ring segment 34 has a second hinge-end drive face 46 and a second latch-end drive face 50. The first hinge-end drive face 44 and the second hinge-end drive face 46 are complementary to one another, and the first latch-end drive face 48 and the second latch-end drive face 50 are complementary to one another. Closing the first frame half 12 and the second frame half 14 brings the first hinge-end drive face 44 into engagement with the second hinge-end drive face 46 at the hinge end and brings the first latch-end drive face 48 into engagement with the second latch-end drive face 50 at the latch end, and thereby couples the first cam-ring segment 32 and the second cam-ring segment 34 so that they rotate together as one cam ring 30 about the central axis of the closed frame.

The first hinge-end drive face 44, the second hinge-end drive face 46, the first latch-end drive face 48 and the second latch-end drive face 50 are shown in FIG. 6. In the closed frame each pair of complementary drive faces transmits circumferential drive from one cam-ring segment to the other, and the complementary drive faces separate when the first frame half 12 and the second frame half 14 are swung apart about the hinge 16.

Each jaw carriage 22 carries a follower 40 that is received in a respective oblique slot 38 of the cam ring 30. The follower 40 and the oblique slot 38 are shown in FIG. 5. Each oblique slot 38 is inclined to the circumferential direction of the cam ring 30, so that rotation of the cam ring 30 through the limited stroke displaces the follower 40, and with it the jaw carriage 22, along the radial guide 24. The oblique slots 38 are arranged so that rotation of the coupled cam ring 30 through the limited stroke produces the same inward travel at every jaw carriage 22. The inward travel of the three jaw carriages 22 is thereby synchronized.

The latch drive

The releasable latch 18 carries a wedge 52, as shown in FIG. 4. Moving the releasable latch 18 drives the wedge 52 against a ring drive face 54 of the cam ring 30, shown in FIGS. 3 and 4, and rotates the coupled cam ring 30 through the limited stroke.

The releasable latch 18 has a first position and a second position. In the first position the releasable latch 18 holds the first frame half 12 and the second frame half 14 around the pipe 20 without clamping the pipe 20. The second position is an over-center position, in which the releasable latch 18 has driven the wedge 52 and the cam ring 30 and clamps the pipe 20.

Referring again to FIG. 4, the releasable latch 18 comprises a latch handle 56, a latch housing 58 and a force-limiting spring stack 62. The force-limiting spring stack 62 lies in a drive path between the latch handle 56 and the wedge 52.

Once all of the jaw pads 26 have contacted the pipe 20, further movement of the latch handle 56 toward the over-center position compresses the force-limiting spring stack 62 rather than forcing one jaw pad 26 farther than the others. The latch travel that remains after contact is therefore taken up by the force-limiting spring stack 62.

An indicator pin 64 is visible on the releasable latch 18 and moves relative to a fixed edge 60 of the latch housing 58 when the force-limiting spring stack 62 enters a predetermined compression range. The position of the indicator pin 64 relative to the fixed edge 60 is a mechanical report that the split pipe clamp 10 is loaded within that range. The indicator pin 64 reports clamp loading and does not measure the diameter of the pipe 20.

Installation and removal

To install the split pipe clamp 10, an operator opens the releasable latch 18 and swings the first frame half 12 and the second frame half 14 apart about the hinge 16. The operator places the frame halves around the pipe 20 and closes them, which brings the first hinge-end drive face 44 into engagement with the second hinge-end drive face 46 and the first latch-end drive face 48 into engagement with the second latch-end drive face 50, and so couples the first cam-ring segment 32 and the second cam-ring segment 34 into a single cam ring 30. The operator then moves the releasable latch 18 to the first position, in which the frame halves are held around the pipe 20 without clamping. The operator then moves the releasable latch 18 to the over-center position. The wedge 52 bears on the ring drive face 54 and rotates the coupled cam ring 30 through the limited stroke, the oblique slots 38 advance all of the jaw carriages 22 inward together, and the jaw pads 26 pivot and seat on the pipe 20. Latch travel remaining after the jaw pads 26 seat compresses the force-limiting spring stack 62, and the operator observes the indicator pin 64 relative to the fixed edge 60.

To remove the split pipe clamp 10, the operator releases the releasable latch 18. The force-limiting spring stack 62 unloads and the carriage springs 42 retract the jaw carriages 22, lifting the jaw pads 26 off the pipe 20 before the frame halves are opened. The first frame half 12 and the second frame half 14 are then swung apart about the hinge 16, and the first hinge-end drive face 44, the second hinge-end drive face 46, the first latch-end drive face 48 and the second latch-end drive face 50 separate so that the first cam-ring segment 32 and the second cam-ring segment 34 travel with their own frame halves. The split pipe clamp 10 is reusable.

The essential relationship of the arrangement described here is that closure of the frame halves couples the cam-ring segments, that travel of the releasable latch 18 drives the coupled cam ring 30, that the oblique slots 38 synchronize the radial travel of the jaw carriages 22, and that the force-limiting spring stack 62 accommodates the remaining latch travel.
