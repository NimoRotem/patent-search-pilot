1. A split pipe clamp comprising:

a first frame half and a second frame half joined at a hinge end by a hinge and releasably joined at a latch end by a releasable latch, the first frame half and the second frame half defining a frame that surrounds a central axis when closed;

three jaw carriages distributed circumferentially about the frame, each jaw carriage being slidable in a respective radial guide of the frame and carrying a jaw pad that is mounted on the jaw carriage for pivoting movement and that presents a concave bearing surface toward the central axis;

a respective carriage spring biasing each jaw carriage outward from the central axis;

a cam ring retained in an annular guide of the frame for rotation about the central axis through a limited stroke, the cam ring comprising a first cam-ring segment carried by the first frame half and a second cam-ring segment carried by the second frame half, the first cam-ring segment and the second cam-ring segment having complementary drive faces at the hinge end and complementary drive faces at the latch end that engage one another when the first frame half and the second frame half are closed and couple the first cam-ring segment and the second cam-ring segment for rotation together as the cam ring;

a follower carried by each jaw carriage and received in a respective oblique slot of the cam ring, each oblique slot being inclined to a circumferential direction of the cam ring such that rotation of the coupled cam ring through the limited stroke advances the jaw carriages inward together along the radial guides;

a wedge carried by the releasable latch and positioned to bear on a ring drive face of the cam ring such that movement of the releasable latch rotates the coupled cam ring through the limited stroke; and

a force-limiting spring stack lying in a drive path between a latch handle of the releasable latch and the wedge.

2. The split pipe clamp of claim 1, wherein the force-limiting spring stack compresses when the latch handle is moved further after all of the jaw pads have contacted a pipe.

3. The split pipe clamp of claim 2, wherein latch travel remaining after all of the jaw pads have contacted the pipe is taken up by the force-limiting spring stack without advancing any one of the jaw pads farther than the others.

4. The split pipe clamp of claim 1, wherein the releasable latch further comprises a latch housing and an indicator pin that moves relative to a fixed edge of the latch housing when the force-limiting spring stack enters a predetermined compression range.

5. The split pipe clamp of claim 1, wherein the releasable latch has a first position in which the releasable latch holds the first frame half and the second frame half closed without clamping and a second, over-center position in which the wedge has rotated the coupled cam ring through the limited stroke.

6. The split pipe clamp of claim 1, wherein the oblique slots are arranged such that rotation of the coupled cam ring through the limited stroke produces the same inward travel at every jaw carriage.

7. The split pipe clamp of claim 1, wherein the complementary drive faces at the hinge end and the complementary drive faces at the latch end separate when the first frame half and the second frame half are swung apart about the hinge, the first cam-ring segment remaining carried by the first frame half and the second cam-ring segment remaining carried by the second frame half.

8. The split pipe clamp of claim 1, wherein the frame carries a mounting boss outside the annular guide for supporting one of a sensor, a tool and a conduit, the mounting boss not participating in synchronizing the jaw carriages.

9. The split pipe clamp of claim 1, wherein the carriage spring of each jaw carriage retracts that jaw carriage outward and lifts the jaw pad carried by it off a pipe when the releasable latch is released.

10. The split pipe clamp of claim 1, wherein each jaw pad, by its pivoting movement on the jaw carriage that carries it, bears on a pipe within the frame without requiring a tool specific pipe diameter.

a pipe; and

13. The installed clamp assembly of claim 11, wherein the frame carries a mounting boss outside the annular guide for supporting one of a sensor, a tool and a conduit.

14. A method of clamping a pipe with a split pipe clamp having a first frame half and a second frame half joined at a hinge end by a hinge and releasably joined at a latch end by a releasable latch, three jaw carriages each slidable in a respective radial guide and each carrying a follower and a jaw pad mounted for pivoting movement, and a cam ring retained in an annular guide and comprising a first cam-ring segment carried by the first frame half and a second cam-ring segment carried by the second frame half, the method comprising:

opening the releasable latch and swinging the first frame half and the second frame half apart about the hinge;

placing the first frame half and the second frame half around the pipe;

closing the first frame half and the second frame half about the pipe, thereby engaging complementary drive faces of the first cam-ring segment and the second cam-ring segment at the hinge end and at the latch end and coupling the first cam-ring segment and the second cam-ring segment for rotation together as the cam ring;

moving the releasable latch to a first position in which the releasable latch holds the first frame half and the second frame half around the pipe without clamping the pipe;

moving the releasable latch to an over-center position, thereby driving a wedge of the releasable latch against a ring drive face of the cam ring and rotating the coupled cam ring through a limited stroke;

advancing the jaw carriages inward together along the radial guides, respective oblique slots of the cam ring displacing the followers so that the jaw pads pivot and seat on the pipe; and

compressing a force-limiting spring stack lying in a drive path between a latch handle of the releasable latch and the wedge with latch travel remaining after the jaw pads have seated on the pipe.

15. The method of claim 14, further comprising observing an indicator pin move relative to a fixed edge of a latch housing of the releasable latch when the force-limiting spring stack enters a predetermined compression range.

16. The method of claim 14, further comprising releasing the releasable latch, retracting the jaw carriages outward under a respective carriage spring biasing each jaw carriage outward so that the jaw pads lift off the pipe, and thereafter swinging the first frame half and the second frame half apart about the hinge so that the complementary drive faces separate and each of the first cam-ring segment and the second cam-ring segment travels with its own frame half.

17. The method of claim 14, further comprising supporting one of a sensor, a tool and a conduit on a mounting boss carried by the split pipe clamp outside the annular guide.
