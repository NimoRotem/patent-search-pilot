View: A front view of the cam ring, shown as if the clamp were closed. Patent line drawing, black on white, no shading or color. The ring is drawn at one size that keeps all line work well inside the drawing area, clear of the margins.

Geometry to draw:
- An annular cam ring, shown schematically, is centered in the drawing area.
- The ring is divided into an upper arcuate segment and a lower arcuate segment by junctions on the left and right.
- Three inclined, elongated slots are spaced around the ring.
- A short drive face is shown schematically on the ring band. Its position along the band is a drawing convention adopted only so that its leader does not collide with the other leaders, and conveys no particular circumferential relationship to the slots or to the junctions.
- The junctions, slots, and drive face are shown schematically.

Numeral placement targets:
- 30: the ring band, its leader terminating at a point clearly spaced from the terminations of the 32 and 34 leaders and clear of the slots, the junctions and the drive face.
- 32: the band of the upper arcuate segment, clear of the slots and of both junctions.
- 34: the band of the lower arcuate segment, clear of the slots and of both junctions.
