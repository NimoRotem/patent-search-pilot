View: enlarged detail view of a single jaw carriage and the adjacent portion of the cam ring, seen along the pipe axis as in FIG. 2. Patent line drawing, black on white, no shading or color. All line work lies well inside the drawing area, clear of the margins.

Geometry to draw:

- A portion of the frame is shown as two parallel radial lines defining a radial guide, running upright and ending well inside the drawing area at both ends.
- A carriage drawn as a radially long rectangle whose two sides run along the two parallel lines of the radial guide, so that the carriage is guided by it.
- On the inner (lower) end of the carriage, a pad drawn as a small block with a concave inner edge, joined to the carriage at a small circle.
- A follower drawn as a small circle carried by the carriage and spaced along it from the pad.
- A portion of the cam ring is shown as a narrow arcuate band crossing the carriage at the follower.
- An elongated slot through the band, centered on the follower, inclined so its long direction is neither purely radial nor purely along the band.
- A spring drawn as a short zigzag line acting between the carriage and the frame, the zigzag lying parallel to the radial guide so that its line of action is radial and biases the carriage outward, toward the upper end of the radial guide.

The carriage, pad, follower, slot, and spring are shown schematically.
