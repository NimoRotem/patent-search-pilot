# FIG. 1

A three-quarter perspective view of the whole platform standing on its support surface, seen from above and from the front left, so that the front and left sides are both visible. All line work stays well inside the drawing area, clear of the filing margins.


A support surface 64 occupies the lower part of the drawing area as a wide horizontal slab in perspective, extending past the platform on the visible sides, shown schematically.

A base frame 10 rests on the upper face of the support surface 64. It is a closed rectangular frame drawn as a low box in perspective, its front and left walls visible as solid faces, shown schematically.

Four upright guide towers 14 rise from the base frame 10, one near each of its corners. Each guide tower 14 is a tall hollow upright column of the same height, its lower end joined to the base frame 10, shown schematically. The two rear guide towers 14 are partly occluded by the two front ones.

An equipment deck 12 is a flat rectangular slab held above the upper ends of the guide towers 14 and parallel to the base frame 10, shown schematically.

Four corner posts 16 depend from the corners of the underside of the equipment deck 12 and run vertically down into the four guide towers 14. Each corner post 16 is a straight upright column, shown schematically, visible over the open gap between the deck underside and the upper end of the guide tower 14 it enters, and occluded below that upper end by the wall of the guide tower 14.

A removable cover 60 is a plain rectangular panel on a wall of the platform below the equipment deck 12, shown schematically.

A release handle 52 is an elongated straight lever projecting outward from a wall of the base frame 10, the visible portion of the release handle 52 running outward from that wall to a free outer end, its inner end being concealed within the base frame 10, shown schematically. The release handle 52 is shown in its pulled position.

A hand crank 36 is drawn as a short horizontal shaft entering a wall of the base frame 10, a radial arm at the outer end of that shaft, and a short grip parallel to the shaft at the far end of the arm, shown schematically. The hand crank 36 is shown in place in the platform.
