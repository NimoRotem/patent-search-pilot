# FIG. 5

A schematic diagram of the release linkage of the platform, laid out in one plane, with the four locking pawls toward the outer corners of the drawing area and the release handle toward the lower part. All line work stays well inside the drawing area, clear of the filing margins.


A base frame 10 is drawn as a plain rectangular boundary outline occupying most of the drawing area, shown schematically.

Four locking pawls 40 are four small rectangular blocks, one near each inner corner of the base frame 10, shown schematically.

A release balance bar 48 is a single elongated straight bar lying across the middle of the drawing area between the four blocks, mounted on the base frame 10, shown schematically.

A balance bar pivot 50 is a small circle with a centre mark at the junction of the inner end of the release handle 52 and the release balance bar 48, the release balance bar 48 being able to turn slightly relative to the release handle 52 about that circle, shown schematically.

A release handle 52 is a straight lever whose inner end meets the release balance bar 48 at the balance bar pivot 50 and which runs from there toward the lower part of the drawing area, crossing the boundary outline and ending at a free outer end outside it, shown schematically.

Four release cables 46 are four single curved paths of uniform thickness. Each path begins at one of four anchor points on the release balance bar 48 and runs outward to one of the four blocks, shown schematically.

A cable adjuster 62 is drawn in each of the four curved paths as a short barrel shape interrupting the path.
