# FIG. 6

A schematic detail view of the crank interlock of the platform, laid out in one plane, showing the crank socket, the hand crank inserted in it, the release handle, and the mechanical interlock that couples the two. All line work stays well inside the drawing area, clear of the filing margins.


A crank socket 38 is drawn near the middle of the drawing area as a short sleeve seen from the side, with one open end that receives the hand crank 36, shown schematically.

A hand crank 36 is drawn as a short straight shank aligned with the crank socket 38 and entering its open end, a radial arm at the outer end of the shank, and a short grip parallel to the shank at the far end of the arm, shown schematically.

A release handle 52 is drawn beside the crank socket 38 as an elongated straight lever, one end fragmentary and closed by a short irregular break line, shown schematically. The release handle 52 is shown in its pulled position.

A mechanical interlock 58 is drawn at the crank socket 38 as a small body, and a straight link line joins that small body to the release handle 52, shown schematically.
