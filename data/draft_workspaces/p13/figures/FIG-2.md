# FIG. 2

A schematic plan view looking straight down on the platform with the equipment deck omitted for clarity, so that the base frame, the guide towers at its corners, the racks, the pinions, and the two transverse shafts lie in the plane of the drawing. All line work stays well inside the drawing area, clear of the filing margins.


A base frame 10 is drawn as a plain rectangular boundary outline occupying most of the drawing area, shown schematically.

A guide tower 14 is drawn as a small square block at each of the four inner corners of the base frame 10, shown schematically.

A corner post 16 is drawn inside each guide tower 14 as a smaller square block, shown schematically.

A rack 18 is drawn on the side of each corner post 16 that faces the interior of the base frame 10 and that confronts the pinion 20 fixed to the transverse shaft at that guide tower 14, as a short plain band along that side, the rack extending vertically along the corner post 16 in the direction of travel so that its teeth are not resolved in this plan view, shown schematically.

A pinion 20 is drawn at each guide tower 14 as a small wheel seen edge-on, that is, as a short bar lying across the transverse shaft to which it is fixed and running from front to rear, one end of that bar meeting the rack 18 of the corner post 16 in that guide tower 14.

A front transverse shaft 22 is a straight line of uniform thickness running across the lower part of the drawing area from the pinion 20 at the lower left guide tower 14 to the pinion 20 at the lower right guide tower 14, both of those pinions 20 being fixed to it.

A rear transverse shaft 24 is a straight line of the same thickness running across the upper part of the drawing area from the pinion 20 at the upper left guide tower 14 to the pinion 20 at the upper right guide tower 14, both of those pinions 20 being fixed to it.

A section line 4-4 is drawn as a broken straight line lying on a vertical cutting plane perpendicular to the rack 18 of the lower left corner post 16 and offset along the front transverse shaft 22 from the pinion 20 at that guide tower 14, the cutting plane lying on the vertical plane through the locking pawl of that guide tower, the broken line running from front to rear across the lower left guide tower 14 and the corner post 16 within it, its lower endpoint in the open area outside the base frame 10 in front of that guide tower 14 and its upper endpoint inside the base frame 10 behind that guide tower 14, with an arrow at each of the two endpoints pointing toward the left side of the drawing area to give the viewing direction and the designation 4-4 placed at both endpoints.
