A height-adjustable equipment platform has a base frame carrying upright guide towers and an equipment deck carrying posts sliding in them. Each post carries a rack, each tower a pinion meshing with that rack, and a synchronizing transmission couples the pinions so one rotary input moves every post equally and the deck stays parallel to the base frame. A spring-biased locking pawl in each tower engages the rack of the post sliding in it, so the standing load passes through the engaged pawls and the towers rather than through the transmission alone. Release cables connect the pawls to a release balance bar pivoted by a release handle and itself pivotable relative to it, so cable-length differences cannot leave one pawl engaged while the others release. A locked-state flag shows through a window of its tower only at full engagement.
