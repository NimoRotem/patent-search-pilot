1. A height-adjustable equipment platform, comprising:

a base frame;

an equipment deck;

at least three upright guide towers carried by the base frame;

a plurality of posts carried by the equipment deck, one post sliding vertically in each guide tower;

a rack carried by each of the posts and extending along that post;

a pinion mounted in each guide tower and meshing with the rack of the post sliding in that guide tower, whereby rotation of the pinion raises and lowers that post;

a synchronizing transmission coupling the pinions of the guide towers for rotation together;

a rotary input comprising a worm input shaft and a worm wheel driven by the worm input shaft and fixed to a transverse shaft of the synchronizing transmission, the rotary input being arranged to drive the synchronizing transmission, whereby rotation of the rotary input moves every post by the same distance and the equipment deck remains parallel to the base frame;

a locking pawl carried by each guide tower and mounted to pivot between a locked position engaging the rack of the post sliding in that guide tower and a released position clear of that rack;

a compression spring biasing each locking pawl toward the locked position, a load of the equipment deck at each post being carried through the locking pawl carried by the guide tower in which that post slides, with that locking pawl in the locked position, and through that guide tower;

a release balance bar mounted on the base frame;

a release cable connecting the release balance bar to each locking pawl; and

a release handle acting on the release balance bar, whereby pulling the release handle tensions the release cables and moves all of the locking pawls to the released positions;

wherein the release balance bar is pivotable relative to the release handle, whereby a difference in effective length between the release cables is taken up by pivoting of the release balance bar and no locking pawl remains engaged with its rack while the remaining locking pawls are in the released positions.

2. The platform of claim 1, wherein a mesh between the worm input shaft and the worm wheel resists back-driving of the synchronizing transmission by the load of the equipment deck while the locking pawls in the locked positions carry that load.

3. The platform of claim 1, wherein the base frame is rectangular, the guide towers are four upright guide towers, one near each corner of the base frame, and each post is a corner post depending from a corner of the equipment deck.
