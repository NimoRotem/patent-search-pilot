# FIG. 4

A flat process flow diagram in plain black line work on white, no shading and no words other than the reference numerals below and the label 'A', all line work clear of the filing margins.

A column of five empty shapes with blank interiors is arranged vertically in the center of the drawing area. The shapes are evenly spaced.
The first, second, third, and fourth shapes from the top are rectangles.
The fifth shape from the top is a diamond.

A sequence of vertical solid lines connects the shapes. Each line has an arrowhead at its lower end. The path leaves the lower vertex of each shape and enters the top vertex of the next shape below it. This connects the first rectangle to the second, the second to the third, the third to the fourth, and the fourth to the fifth (the diamond).

A line leaves the left vertex of the fifth shape (the diamond) and runs left. The line rises alongside the column, then runs right at the mid height of the topmost rectangle into one arrowhead touching the left-hand side of that rectangle.

From the lower vertex of the fifth shape (the diamond), a line with an arrowhead points downward to a small empty circle. The circle must contain the capital letter 'A' as a text label. The circle indicates continuation of the process in FIG. 5.

Part names below are semantic identifiers only and do not appear as drawing text. Each numeral sits in the white space beside the column, joined to the shape it names by a short straight leader that ends well inside that shape and crosses no other leader and no line work.

## Numerals shown on this figure

- 202 available current determination step: leader ends inside the topmost rectangle.
- 204 sustaining and deficit assignment step: leader ends inside the second rectangle from the top.
- 206 pilot command step: leader ends inside the third rectangle from the top.
- 208 connector verification step: leader ends inside the fourth rectangle from the top.
- 209 branch overcurrent detection step: leader ends inside the fifth shape, which is the uppermost diamond.
