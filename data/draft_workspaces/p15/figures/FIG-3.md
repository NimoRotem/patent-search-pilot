# FIG. 3

A flat block diagram of the edge controller in plain black line work on white. The drawing area contains no shading and no words or text labels, only the reference numerals listed below. All line work is to stay clear of the filing margins.

One large rectangle, the edge controller 106, occupies the left and central portion of the drawing area.

A further empty rectangle, the local fault indicator 116, stands outside the large rectangle, far to its right in the upper right region. A short solid line runs from the right side of the large rectangle to the left side of the local fault indicator rectangle.

A short solid line extends upward from the middle of the upper side of the large rectangle. This line is the connection to the branch current sensor. It is a straight vertical segment whose lower end is on the upper side of the large rectangle and whose upper end is free.

A short solid line extends downward from the middle of the lower side of the large rectangle. This line is the connection to the isolated local bus. It is a straight vertical segment whose upper end is on the lower side of the large rectangle and whose lower end is free.

The part names below are semantic identifiers only and do not appear as drawing text. Each numeral sits clear of the line work on a short straight leader that crosses no other leader and no line work.

## Numerals shown on this figure

- 106 edge controller: leader ends on the boundary of the large rectangle.
- 116 local fault indicator: leader ends well inside the rectangle right of the large rectangle.
