# FIG. 5

A flat process flow diagram in plain black line work on white, no shading and no words other than the reference numerals below, all line work clear of the filing margins.

The diagram continues from FIG. 4, starting with a small empty circle labeled 'A' at the top center of the drawing area.

A column of five empty shapes with blank interiors is arranged vertically in the center of the drawing area, below the starting connector 'A'. The shapes are evenly spaced.
The first shape below the connector is a rectangle.
The second shape is a diamond.
The third shape is a rectangle.
The fourth shape is a diamond.
The fifth and bottommost shape is a rectangle.

A vertical solid line with an arrowhead at its lower end joins the connector 'A' to the topmost shape, and then joins each shape to the one below it in sequence, down to the bottommost rectangle, except where a path leaves a side vertex of a diamond. The main path leaves the lower vertex of each shape and enters the top of the next.

A line leaves the left vertex of the second shape from the top (the upper diamond in this figure) and runs left. This line connects to the leftward-running return path shown in FIG. 4.

The line running down to the bottom rectangle leaves the lower vertex of the fourth shape (the lower diamond in this figure). A short horizontal line leaves the right vertex of that diamond, runs right and ends in an arrowhead at a small solid square terminator.

A right return path starts with no arrowhead at the right-hand side of the bottom rectangle, at its mid height, runs right, rises clear of the square terminator and of the whole column, and turns left, indicating a return to the start of the process in FIG. 4.

Part names below are semantic identifiers only and do not appear as drawing text. Each numeral sits in the white space beside the column, joined to the shape it names by a short straight leader that ends well inside that shape and crosses no other leader and no line work.

## Numerals shown on this figure

- 210 reduced pilot command sending step: leader ends inside the first shape, a rectangle.
- 211 overcurrent persistence verification step: leader ends inside the second shape, a diamond.
- 212 ordered shedding step: leader ends inside the third shape, a rectangle.
- 214 welded-contactor detection step: leader ends inside the fourth shape, a diamond.
- 216 conditional reclosure step: leader ends inside the bottommost rectangle.
