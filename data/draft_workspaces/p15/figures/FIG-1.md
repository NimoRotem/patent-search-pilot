# FIG. 1

A flat schematic system diagram in plain black line work on white, no shading and no words other than the reference numerals below. All line work stays clear of the filing margins and contains no hop-over arcs.

A dashed rectangle, the charging installation, encloses the whole diagram.

A horizontal solid line, the branch conductor, is drawn across the upper part of the drawing area, inside the dashed rectangle. The left end of the branch conductor stops short of the left side of the dashed rectangle. The right end of the branch conductor meets the right side of the dashed rectangle but does not cross it.

An empty rectangle, the branch current sensor, is drawn in the left portion of the branch conductor; the branch conductor passes through the vertical centre of the branch current sensor.

Three identical empty rectangular blocks are arranged in a row horizontally in the middle of the drawing area, with space between them. Their top edges are at the same height and their bottom edges are at the same height. From left to right, they are: a first connector channel, a second connector channel, and a non-charging load. The branch current sensor is entirely to the left of the first connector channel.

A short vertical line drops from the branch conductor to the top edge of each of the three blocks, connecting them in parallel. All three drops are to the right of the branch current sensor.

An empty rectangular block, the edge controller, is drawn in the lower left of the drawing area, entirely below the three blocks. The right side of the edge controller is to the left of the first connector channel.

A solid horizontal line, the isolated local bus, is drawn in the lower part of the drawing area. It runs from a point vertically below the edge controller to a point vertically below the second connector channel.
A vertical line connects the bottom of the edge controller to the left end of the isolated local bus.
A vertical line connects the bottom of the first connector channel to the isolated local bus.
A vertical line connects the bottom of the second connector channel to the isolated local bus.
The non-charging load is not connected to the isolated local bus.

A solid line joins the branch current sensor to the edge controller. The line runs down from the middle of the bottom side of the branch current sensor, then turns right, then turns down to meet the top side of the edge controller.

## Numerals shown on this figure

- 100 charging installation: leader ends on the dashed enclosing rectangle.
- 102 branch conductor: leader ends on the horizontal line right of the sensor rectangle and clear of the drops.
- 104 branch current sensor: leader ends on the rectangle straddling the horizontal line.
- 106 edge controller: leader ends inside the lower left block, clear of its outline.
- 108 isolated local bus: leader ends on the bus line between the edge controller block and the first branch.
- 118 non-charging load: leader ends well inside the rightmost block.
- 120 first connector channel: leader ends well inside the leftmost block.
- 140 second connector channel: leader ends well inside the middle block.
