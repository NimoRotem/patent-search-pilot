# FIG. 2

Flat schematic of one connector channel in plain black line work on white, no shading and no words other than the reference numerals below, clear of the filing margins. The only dashed work is one dashed rectangle; all other lines are solid.

One dashed rectangle, and exactly one, occupies the left and central portion and is the first connector channel. A solid vertical line enters from above, crosses its top side in the left portion and continues downward inside it as the incoming branch supply. A contactor, a small empty square straddling this line, interrupts it in the upper half of the rectangle.

Below the contactor the line turns right at about mid height and runs horizontally to the left side of an empty rectangular block that straddles the right side of the dashed rectangle and is the vehicle connector. A small empty circle straddles this horizontal run midway along it and is the connector current sensor.

An empty rectangular block in the lower region of the dashed rectangle is the control-pilot interface, wholly below the horizontal run, clear of the circle and left of the vehicle connector block. A solid line leaves the middle of its right side and runs straight to the bottom side of the vehicle connector block.

Right of the dashed rectangle a larger rounded rectangle is an electric vehicle, joined to the right side of the vehicle connector block by a short solid line.

A solid horizontal line enters from the left edge of the drawing area and crosses the left side of the dashed rectangle in its lower region as the isolated local bus, ending at the middle of the control-pilot interface block's left side. A short vertical branch rises from this bus, left of the incoming supply line, to the left side of the contactor square.

A solid line from the connector current sensor circle runs downward and exits the dashed rectangle of the first connector channel 120, indicating a connection independent of the isolated local bus 108.

## Numerals shown on this figure

- 108 isolated local bus: leader ends on the bus line left of the dashed rectangle.
- 120 first connector channel: leader ends on the dashed rectangle.
- 122 contactor: leader ends on the square outline.
- 124 connector current sensor: leader ends on the upper part of the circle.
- 126 control-pilot interface: leader ends well inside that lower block.
- 128 vehicle connector: the numeral sits below the vehicle connector block, with a short leader rising to end well inside it.
- 130 electric vehicle: leader ends well inside the rounded rectangle.
