An apparatus controls charging of electric vehicles at a plurality of vehicle connectors supplied by a common electrical branch whose branch current limit is lower than a sum of the current ratings of those vehicle connectors. Each connector channel of the apparatus includes a contactor connected between the electrical branch and a respective vehicle connector, a connector current sensor arranged to measure a connector current delivered through that vehicle connector, and a control-pilot interface, and each connector channel is associated with a local connector identifier. A branch current sensor is arranged to measure a branch current in the electrical branch upstream of the contactors of the plurality of connector channels, so that the aggregate current of the branch is obtained from a measurement that is independent of the per connector measurements.

A controller is coupled to the control-pilot interfaces, to the connector current sensors, to the contactors and to the branch current sensor. The controller stores, for each active connector channel, a charging authorization, a requested current, a minimum sustaining current and an energy-deficit value. At each allocation interval of a succession of allocation intervals, the controller subtracts a measured non-charging branch load and a configurable safety reserve from the branch current limit to obtain an available charging current, assigns the minimum sustaining current from the available charging current to each active connector channel that can receive the minimum sustaining current, and distributes a remainder of the available charging current among the active connector channels according to the stored energy-deficit values, thereby obtaining an assigned current limit for each active connector channel. The controller sends the assigned current limit for each active connector channel through the control-pilot interface of that connector channel and verifies the connector current resulting from the assigned current limit with the connector current sensor of that connector channel.

The branch current sensor serves as an independent safety input. Responsive to the measured branch current remaining above the branch current limit for a verification interval after a reduced pilot command has been sent, the controller opens the contactors of the plurality of connector channels one at a time in a stored shedding order until the measured branch current is below the branch current limit. Load is therefore removed in a bounded and predetermined sequence, and only to the extent that the measured branch current shows the removal to be necessary.

In further aspects, a contactor is not reclosed until the connector current sensor of its connector channel reports zero connector current, the control-pilot interface of that connector channel reports a connected vehicle, and a randomized recovery delay has expired, so that connector channels that were shed together do not restore load simultaneously. A welded-contactor condition is recorded when the connector current of a connector channel remains above a leakage threshold after an open command has been issued to the contactor of that connector channel, whereupon the controller inhibits the contactors of the other connector channels on the electrical branch and operates a local fault indicator.

Corresponding methods are also disclosed.
