## Summary

This document discloses a magnetic chuck or holding device for gripping a workpiece, which includes a magnetic assembly that moves between an "ON" (gripping) and "OFF" (release) position, and pole-pieces that provide holding effort and cooperate with the magnetic assembly to guide the magnetic field.
