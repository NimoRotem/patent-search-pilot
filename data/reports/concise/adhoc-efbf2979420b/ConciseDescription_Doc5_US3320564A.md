## Summary

This document discloses a magnetic chuck for holding parts, which includes magnetic pressings, a pole ring, and a locating pin for positioning workpieces, and a mechanical means for removing parts.

## Claim chart

The reference discloses a magnetic chuck for holding parts, which is a magnetic gripper for a ferromagnetic workpiece.

> This invention relates to magnetic chucks and, more particularly, to an improved magnetic chuck in combination with a removing means for removing parts therefrom.
