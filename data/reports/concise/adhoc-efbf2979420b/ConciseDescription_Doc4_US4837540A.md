## Summary

This document discloses a magnetic work-holder that is a magnetic holding device with pole members and single-pole extension members, including a movable pole portion with a work-holding face and guiding means, where slanted abutting faces form an angle with the holding surface.

## Claim chart

### Claim 1: "A magnetic gripper for gripping a ferromagnetic workpiece, said magnetic gripper comprising"

The reference discloses a magnetic holding device comprising a magnetically activable holding surface having pole members of different polarities, and at least a first and a second single-pole extension members for said pole members of opposite polarities.

> A magnetic holding device, comprising magnetically activable holding surface having pole members of different polarities, and at least a first and a second single-pole extension members for said pole members of opposite polarities.
