## Summary

This document discloses a magnetic lifting device for manipulating metal plates, which includes a horseshoe magnet with spaced magnetic legs, non-magnetic clips attached to the magnet legs, and a pusher bar for aligning workpieces, where magnetic contact can be broken by a rocking movement.
