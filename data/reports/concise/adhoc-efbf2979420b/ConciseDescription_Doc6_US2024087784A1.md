## Summary

This document discloses a magnetic coupling device, which may include sensors, retractable pins, stationary pins, probes, and/or additional tools, for magnetically coupling to a ferromagnetic workpiece.

## Claim chart

- Claim 1

### Claim 1: "a magnet which is arranged in the housing and which is displaceable along a displacement direction between a gripping position for gripping the ferromagnetic workpiece and a release position for releasing the ferromagnetic workpiece"

The reference discloses a switchable magnetic flux source supported by the housing, including a plurality of permanent magnets, where a first permanent magnet and a second permanent magnet are movable relative to each other.
