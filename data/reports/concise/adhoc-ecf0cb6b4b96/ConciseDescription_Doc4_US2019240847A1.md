- Title: Gripping device having a bernoulli gripping unit and a vacuum gripping unit

## Summary

This document discloses a gripping device for an end effector of a manipulator for holding a workpiece via its workpiece surface, having a Bernoulli gripping unit and a vacuum gripping unit, where the Bernoulli negative pressure region and the vacuum negative pressure region intersect at least in part or surround one another.
